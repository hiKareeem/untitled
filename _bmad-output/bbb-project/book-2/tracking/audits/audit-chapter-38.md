**Bakker alignment:** Extended, philosophically dense constructions present in the theological passages — the circulation insight section uses subordinated clauses and long compound sentences consistent with the Bakker register. The pastoral/quiet sections (opening, Imani exchanges) are shorter and more clipped. Register shifts track the arc: ordinary → overwhelm → theological density → return to terse exchange. ✅
