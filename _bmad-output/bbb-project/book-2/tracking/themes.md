## Core Themes (Book 2)

Based on trilogy plan and Book 1 continuation:

1. **Radicalization vs. Reform** — The survivors choose different paths
2. **Truth Without Power** — Evidence exists, institutions ignore it
3. **The Absorbed as Political Question** — What are they? Who speaks for them?
4. **Complicity's Aftermath** — Living with what you authorized/maintained/witnessed
5. **Institutional Collapse vs. Continuity** — Systems that failed continue functioning
