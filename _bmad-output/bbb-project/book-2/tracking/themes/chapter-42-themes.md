## Key Thematic Resonances

**Architecture of Power ↔ Institutional Betrayal:** The building that cannot repair a holographic emitter transmits the voices of the dead through its own speaker system. The institution that has no framework for the absorbed transmits Mirelle's continued report through the infrastructure designed to amplify delegates. The architecture of power is not selective — it amplifies what it receives. The betrayal is that the institution receives Mirelle's transmission, amplifies it, notes it in the record, and continues with Item Five. The architecture of power is not the institution's malice. It is its indifference.

**Truth as Weapon ↔ Systemic Complicity:** The truth is in the record. The record is the institution's terminal function. The institution converts truth into a procedural item. The weapon is filed. The complicity is not suppression — it is the specific institutional act of receiving truth, timestamping it, and advancing the agenda. The record contains Mirelle. The record will not act on Mirelle. The truth is weaponless inside the institution that holds it.

**Where Identity Ends ↔ What We Owe:** Mirelle is still Mirelle. The debt is to the person, not the record. Zeyad places the recorder on the clerk's table and speaks three sentences because Mirelle is still doing her job and the record should contain it. The debt is not to the institution. The debt is to the journalist who is still filing from inside the void, who is still doing the work, who is still Mirelle. The record is the only instrument Zeyad has. The record contains her.
