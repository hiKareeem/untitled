## Key Thematic Resonances

**Architecture of Power ↔ Systemic Complicity:** The VS-7 dropdown converts Nikolai's potential honesty into institutional deviation before he types a word. The architecture of complicity is not the institution's demand that he lie — it is the institution's inability to process the truth. The form's categories are the complicity mechanism. The free-text override is the first time Nikolai has found a gap in the architecture large enough to put a true thing through. The gap is one field. The truth enters through the unmonitored field and the system flags it as an anomaly.

**Progress Requires Atrocity ↔ Where Identity Ends:** The FAS approach's zero secondary absorption events answers both themes simultaneously. No new absorbed. The existing absorbed treated as present, not casualties. The FAS doctrine assumes the breach responds — which assumes the breach is a system, not an enemy. If the breach is a system, the absorbed are not casualties. They are people inside a system. The FAS form's *altered state* classification is the institutional acknowledgment of this. The PCC's NRC classification is the institutional denial of it.

**What We Owe ↔ Hope as Resistance:** The field log convergence is both the chapter's most disorienting moment and its most hopeful. The private record and the official record say the same thing. The field log has no jurisdiction. The man who lived in the jurisdiction has nowhere to stand. But the honest report is in the system. The truth is filed. The hope is not that it will change anything. The hope is that it exists.
