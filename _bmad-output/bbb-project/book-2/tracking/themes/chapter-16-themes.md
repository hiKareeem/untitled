## Detailed Thematic Analysis

### Where Identity Ends (Dominant)

The chapter tracks Kira through three identity states in sequence:

1. **Expelled mage** — The letter confirms what she already was. The institution catches up. "The letter was the institution filing *her.*" Identity defined by institutional removal.

2. **Lexicon-builder** — The Idris call transforms her from data architect to language architect. "The filing that had become a dataset. The dataset was becoming a language." The filing habit (coping mechanism, B1 Ch 10) → dataset (B2 Ch 6) → vocabulary/language (B2 Ch 16). Each evolution is an identity shift: from managing to naming to building.

3. **Person making a choice** — "This was for her." Neo-Shanghai is the first thing she does for herself, not the network. The streaming-freedom realization ("the ring light could come back on") adds a fourth: she can be a public person again, on her own terms.

The epigraph seeds this: "those who named it and those who listened" — Kira is discovering she is one of the namers. The naming IS the identity.

### Truth as Weapon (Strong)

Two weapon systems in this chapter:

1. **Sofia's publication** — External validation. Kira's 14-month telemetry dataset cited anonymously, given institutional weight by a VEC researcher. "Made real by someone else's credentials." The weapon is legitimacy — the same data, repackaged with authority, breaks the permission barrier for 167 new mages.

2. **The emerging vocabulary** — Internal weapon. Kira and Idris building words for what the signal does: "phrasing," "cadence," "communication," "directed." The "Phenomenological character" field is the formalization — giving the network permission to describe experience, not just measure it. "There is no correct terminology. The terminology is being built." This is a weapon against institutional language ("implant resonance feedback consistent with known atmospheric conditions").

### Architecture of Power (Strong)

The chapter reads institutions as architecture:

- The expulsion letter: 12 paragraphs, 6 of throat-clearing. "It told her more about the Academy than it told her about herself."
- The grounding paradox: expelled from the institution that grounded her = grounding dies. Power structures contain their own dissolution.
- Filing categories as mini-architecture: duration, magnitude, pattern, correlation. Built for management. Insufficient for experience. The addition of "phenomenological character" = rebuilding the architecture from within.
- Imani's message bypassing Ren's chain: institutional chains (Ren→contact→circle→Nephthys) vs. direct action (Nephthys→Imani→Kira). Speed as power architecture.
