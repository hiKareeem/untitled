## Structural Constraints (for Opus window)

1. **No consecutive same-POV** — maintained from Book 1.
2. **Class contrast** — alternate Spire/institutional (Aurielle, Malachi, Sofia, Zeyad) with ground-level/grassroots (Nephthys, Kira, Nikolai, Nuwa, Fuxi) for socioeconomic register variety.
3. **Mumbai convergence (Ch 30-33):** Consecutive dedicated chapters, different temporal distances from the event, NOT simultaneous cross-cutting like BLACKWEIR. Design session Decision #9.
4. **Nephthys-Kira scene:** Phase 2 or early Phase 3. Currently placed at Ch 21 (Kira POV). Design session Decision #10.
5. **AEGIS debut:** Phase 2 (Ch 19). Present-tense mid-truth processing. NOT BLACKWEIR callback. Design session Decision #8.
6. **Fuxi's coda:** Phase 1 only (Ch 9). After this, Fuxi appears only through Nuwa's lens. Design session Decision #1.
7. **Malachi debut:** Phase 1 (Ch 3). Early placement to establish the institutional theology perspective before the mid-truth lands.
8. **Book 2 ends on Nuwa** — the expendable person stepping into visibility. Thematic bookend.
9. **The Voice chapters (Ph4):** Each POV gets exactly one Voice chapter. The event is the same; the experience is different. Slower rhythm than BLACKWEIR. Ontological, not physical.

## Thread Architecture (Book 2)

### Thread A: The Corporate Machine (Aurielle + Thorne)
**Book 1 continuation:** The machine resumes, uglier. SHEPHERD (void-exploitation) is Book 2's BLACKWEIR-equivalent corporate decision.
**Active phases:** 1-5
**Key beat:** SHEPHERD is not crisis response — it's business model. BLACKWEIR industrialized.

### Thread B: The Investigation → The Truth (Sofia + Mirelle's legacy)
**Book 1 continuation:** Mirelle is gone. Sofia inherits. The mid-truth assembly (Phase 2) is the thread's climax.
**Active phases:** 1-5 (Mirelle diegetic only: dead-hand, voice in the Voice)
**Key beat:** Truth goes public. World adapts around it. Correct and insufficient.

### Thread C: The Sump → The Invisible (Nuwa + Fuxi coda)
**Book 1 continuation:** Fuxi radicalized, Nuwa invisible. Fuxi's coda chapter closes his arc. Nuwa carries the thread.
**Active phases:** 1-5 (Fuxi: 1 only)
**Key beat:** Sacrifice district designation = the Sump made global policy. Nuwa steps out.

### Thread D: The Theology (Nephthys + Malachi)
**Book 1 expansion:** Split into two POVs. Same fracture, opposite sides. Grassroots prophet vs. institutional suppressor.
**Active phases:** 1-5
**Key beat:** Church fracture (Listeners/Witnesses/Threshold). Malachi can't hold the center. Nephthys can't control the edges.

### Thread E: The Military (Nikolai + AEGIS)
**Book 1 expansion:** Nikolai's doctrine meets AEGIS's automation. Mumbai makes the parallel explicit.
**Active phases:** 1-5 (Nikolai), 2-4 (AEGIS)
**Key beat:** Mumbai — AEGIS does what Nikolai was trained to do, faster and worse. Honest report.

### Thread F: The Broadcast (Kira + mage network)
**Book 1 continuation:** Scream → network → global relay. Lumina dependency arc.
**Active phases:** 1-5
**Key beat:** Nephthys-Kira scene (two epistemologies meeting). Second broadcast during the Voice.

### Thread G: The Diplomacy (Zeyad + UGC + FAS)
**Book 1 continuation:** The pen continues. Lagos introduces FAS alternative model.
**Active phases:** 1-5
**Key beat:** FAS model proves insufficient. UGC vote passes and is injuncted. The record is ignored.

### NEW Thread H: The Observer (AEGIS)
**New thread:** The non-human POV processing everything the human POVs experience.
**Active phases:** 2-4
**Key beat:** Mumbai autonomous fire. Post-Voice processing. The gap between targeting and something else.

## Open Questions for Opus Window

1. **Prologue/Epilogue?** Book 1 had both. Book 2 may not need a prologue (opens on Aurielle post-BLACKWEIR). Epilogue options: void infrastructure learning, first Threshold volunteer's experience, or end cleanly on Nuwa Ch 52.
2. **Nephthys-Kira scene POV:** Currently sketched as Kira (Ch 21). Could be Nephthys. Could be split. The Opus window should decide based on narrative function.
3. **Mumbai chapter ordering:** Currently AEGIS → Nikolai → Kira → Aurielle. Could reorder for maximum impact. The Opus window should sequence for dramatic irony.
4. **Malachi-Aurielle interaction:** The Church and NitroCore have institutional relationships. Do they share a scene? A communication? The intersection of corporate and theological power.
5. **Malachi-Nephthys communication:** Do they communicate at all in Book 2? Through intermediaries? Through the locked correspondence becoming partially public?
6. **AEGIS BLACKWEIR callback placement:** The design session preserved this material for later chapters as "reprocessing-with-new-context." Where does it go? Phase 3 (pre-Mumbai) as AEGIS reprocessing BLACKWEIR before making the Mumbai decision?
7. **Epigraph system:** Book 1 used 17 named in-world works. Book 2 needs to continue the system — some returning works (A History of Silence, Solberg report, etc.) plus new ones reflecting new POVs (Malachi's correspondence, AEGIS-related documents, post-BLACKWEIR texts).
8. **The Voice timing:** Does the Voice hit mid-Phase 4 or open Phase 4? Currently the first Voice chapter is Ch 36 (second chapter of Phase 4, after a Sofia setup chapter). The Opus window should decide.
9. **Book 2 title:** "The Reckoning" is in the trilogy plan. Confirm or revise.
