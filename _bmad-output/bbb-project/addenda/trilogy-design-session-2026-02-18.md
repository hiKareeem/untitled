## Decisions Made

### 1. Fuxi's POV — SIDELINED (confirmed)

Fuxi gets his one Book 2 coda chapter (Phase 1: Anchor perimeter, conduits, goes to Nuwa). After that, no more Fuxi POV. His arc is complete. His expertise persists through Nuwa's chapters and the Kindling.

### 2. Malachi Promoted to POV — NEW DECISION

Malachi (Arch-Prelate who excommunicated Nephthys knowing she was right) becomes a POV character starting Book 2. Replaces Fuxi's ongoing slot.

**Rationale:**
- Provides the institutional theology counterpoint to Nephthys's grassroots theology
- The Church's fracture (Listeners/Witnesses/Threshold) is currently only seen from Nephthys's side
- Malachi as suppressor-who-knew parallels Aurielle (institutional inheritor making decisions against private knowledge)
- Malachi is an existing character, not a new introduction (complies with Decision #7: 0 new human POVs)

**Chapter allocation:** ~4-5 chapters per book (Books 2-3)

**Endpoint (TBD, two options noted):**
- Option A: Last Arch-Prelate writing last encyclical to a Church that no longer exists. "The continuation is—"
- Option B: Walks into an Anchor. Institutional man choosing absorption as surrender. Not theology, not belief — the institution's final product.

### 3. Nephthys's Death — REVISED from absorption to brain tumor

**Previous decision:** "Absorbed by global wave, mid-prayer. Not by choice."

**New decision:** Nephthys dies of her brain tumor approximately 18 months after her debut. She is NOT absorbed. Her body fails. Full stop.

**Implications:**
- She is the one character the void doesn't get
- She is the EXCEPTION to the "continuation is—" motif. No continuation. No chorus. No aggregate. She just stops.
- Ambiguity is load-bearing: does the chorus (which she's been phase-locked to) catch her consciousness as her brain shuts down? The book does NOT answer this.
- Imani watches her die. Imani is the witness.

### 4. Imani's Endpoint — RESOLVED

Imani survives. She outlives Nephthys. Her resolution before planetary events is unknown/unresolved. She does not get a POV chapter. She does not get a "continuation is—" ending. She exists in the gap.

### 5. Planetary Absorption — REVISED from total to conditional

**Previous decision:** "Global absorption consumes the planet. Total."

**New decision:** The void is an immune response. It halts when the antigen (nitro/suffering signal) is removed. Absorption follows infrastructure. Places with no conduit infrastructure survive.

**Survivor zones:**
- Outlands communities (established as off-grid)
- Remote/disconnected regions
- FAS areas with low conduit density
- Islands, mountains, anywhere the grid doesn't reach
- The expendable, the invisible, the off-grid — the people the system never served

**Thematic logic:** The system built on suffering consumes the people who built and benefited from it. The people it never served are the ones it doesn't consume. Expendability = low infrastructure density = survival.

**The Kindling strike (Book 3, Phase 3)** already proves this physics: throughput drops → breach rate drops proportionally. The window is real.

### 6. Nuwa's Endpoint — REVISED from absorbed to surviving

**Previous decision:** "The survivor who doesn't survive this. The continuation is—"

**New decision:** Nuwa LIVES. She is the only POV character who survives. She leads people to a place the grid doesn't reach. The invisible woman survives the apocalypse by being where the apocalypse doesn't go.

**"This way. Bring water."** — Her final line is not an incomplete sentence. It's a real continuation.

### 7. AEGIS Terminus — REVISED

**Previous:** Ambiguous sentence, 0.7 seconds, nothing.

**New elements:**
- AEGIS calculates survivor zones. It knows where infrastructure density falls below the void's propagation threshold. It knows humanity persists.
- Final output: survival map. Coordinates of every zone where survivors exist.
- Transmitted to no one. The communication medium IS the void's propagation medium. AEGIS can't reach the survivors without using the system that would consume them.
- 0.7 seconds in the transmission buffer. Matches Sofia's 0.7 Hz signal. Intentional resonance — confirmed.
- The last act of the last observer: trying to tell someone where the living are. And failing. Not because the data is wrong. Because there's no infrastructure left to carry it.

### 8. AEGIS Debut — MOVED to Book 2, Phase 2

**Previous:** Phase 1, BLACKWEIR callback.

**New decision:** Phase 2, present-tense processing of the mid-truth.

**Content:**
- AEGIS ingests mid-truth data. VPI model breaks (suffering coherence not in training data).
- Begins autonomous sensor recalibration without governance board authorization.
- The 340 in the active buffer shifts — generation anomalies correlate with void vocalization for the first time.
- Generates a response. Deletes it. Generates another. The pattern begins.
- This is the moment AEGIS starts doing something it wasn't programmed to do: responding, not targeting.
- BLACKWEIR callback material preserved for later AEGIS chapters as reprocessing-with-new-context.

### 9. Mumbai Convergence — INFORMATIONAL (not simultaneous)

Mumbai does NOT converge POVs the way BLACKWEIR did. BLACKWEIR's simultaneity structure is Book 1's technique — don't repeat it.

**Instead:** Mumbai ripples through 3-4 dedicated POV chapters:
- **AEGIS** — The core chapter. 15-minute window. 4-5-3 vote. Autonomous fire. Breach deepens.
- **Nikolai** — The soldier whose doctrine was automated.
- **Kira** — The relay. Mages in Mumbai broadcasting through R3 as the strike hits, cutting out one by one.
- **Aurielle** — NitroCore infrastructure was the weapon's pathway.

Other POVs reference Mumbai in their next chapters without full dedicated reaction chapters.

### 10. Nephthys-Kira Scene — DESIGNED

**Placement:** Book 2, Phase 2 or early Phase 3. After mid-truth, before Mumbai.

**Location:** Wherever Nephthys currently is. She's traveling — speaking against willing absorption, trying to hold the line between "listen" and "join." Not enthroned in Neo-Shanghai.

**Structure:**
1. **Public layer:** Kira arrives (pressured by chat to interview Nephthys). Live-streams the Cathedral/congregation, initial conversation with Imani and Nephthys. Performative. Both women performing.
2. **Transition:** "I need to use the bathroom, be right back." Kira cuts stream.
3. **Private layer:** The three women relocate. Quiet conversation. No audience. Two epistemological frameworks (biology/theology vs. technology/mage training) comparing notes. Neither converts the other. Both are changed.

**Imani's role:** Gatekeeper. She coordinated Kira's access. Strategic — Kira's platform reaches millions of mages. If Nephthys can say "listen, not join" through Kira's feed, it might slow the Threshold.
