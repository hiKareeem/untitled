## Historical Timeline (2031–2175)

> Pre-story events that shaped the 2175 status quo. Characters reference these the way we reference World War II or the moon landing — settled history that shaped the present.

### 2031 — The Cascade Crisis

- **Event:** Simultaneous global energy grid collapse — cascading failures across aging nuclear, solar, and fossil fuel infrastructure
- **Duration:** 11 days
- **Death Toll:** 400,000+
- **Cause:** Combination of cyberattack, extreme weather, and systemic underinvestment
- **Consequence:** Global trauma, economic devastation, desperate hunger for any energy alternative. Creates the political and psychological conditions for nitro's rapid adoption without sufficient scrutiny
- **Cultural Legacy:** People still argue whether it was engineered or organic

### 2038 — First Crude Nitro Capture

- **Event:** Deep-sea research teams discover anomalous fluid deposits at hydrothermal vent sites — luminous, orange, extraordinary energy density
- **Consequence:** Initial characterization takes years. Substance resists easy categorization. Early researchers note the characteristic hum but attribute it to resonance properties

### 2042 — NitroCore Refinement Breakthrough

- **Event:** Proprietary catalytic process successfully stabilizes crude nitro and converts it into grid-compatible energy at scale
- **Consequence:** NitroCore patents the refinement process. First nitro-powered city district goes online. The transition begins

### 2044 — The Nitro Accords

- **Event:** International agreement signed in Geneva — intended to prevent resource wars by opening extraction to all nations while keeping refinement proprietary and licensed
- **Consequence:** In practice, locks ordinary nations out of technology while entrenching patent-holding corporations. Creates the legal framework for corporate sovereignty over nitro infrastructure. Free African States negotiate special provisions maintaining partial energy independence
- **Key Figure:** Zeyad Al-Fahim's grandfather helped draft these accords

### 2050s — The Great Transition

- **Event:** Decade of rapid global infrastructure conversion. Cities rebuild around nitro trunk-lines. Nuclear decommissioned. Solar/battery relegated to backup and rural use
- **Consequence:** Neo-Shanghai's Spires begin construction. The hum becomes ubiquitous. First generation grows up never knowing silence. Catalyst monopoly consolidates — 3-5 corporations control rare earth supply chains essential for refinement

### 2053–2059 — The Resource Wars

- **Event:** Wars fought over rare minerals needed for refinement catalyst fabrication. Central Asia and sub-Saharan Africa destabilized for six years
- **Consequence:** Millions displaced. Free African States survive relatively intact by refusing full nitro integration. Corporate supply chain control consolidated globally. The Kindling labor movement is born in the extraction camps

### 2066 — Corporate Sovereignty Recognition

- **Event:** United Nations framework formally recognizes corporate governance authority within defined operational territories
- **Consequence:** Bureaucratic acknowledgment that corporations already govern more effectively than weakened nation-states. UGC (United Global Council) established as successor to the UN, with corporate seats alongside national representatives. GCTA created as nominal regulatory authority

### ~2080s — Church of the Blessed Nitro Emerges

- **Event:** During the Uplift Protocols era, the Church emerges as a major faith — the hum as sacred sound, nitro as divine provision
- **Consequence:** Becomes a dominant spiritual institution, particularly strong in the Sump where it provides community, meaning, and mutual aid that corporations don't

### 2085 — The Uplift Protocols

- **Event:** Global deployment of nitro technology to developing regions. Poverty effectively eliminated worldwide — basic energy, food production, and manufacturing become trivially cheap
- **Consequence:** Genuine material improvement. Simultaneously cements corporate dependency across the global population. FAS's resistance to full integration becomes increasingly isolated

### 2089 — Hayward Fault Rupture (M7.2)

- **Event:** The earthquake the Bay Area had been waiting for since the 20th century. Hayward Fault rupture devastates the already sea-level-stressed Bay Area. Eastern Bay Bridge span collapses, liquefaction destroys remaining flatland neighborhoods, shoreline infrastructure fails
- **Death toll:** Moderated by nitro-era early warning systems and construction standards, but the physical city is wrecked
- **Consequence:** The PCC, seeking a legislative capital on the eastern Pacific shore — neutral ground between the Asian core and the Americas — invests massively in rebuilding. They don't rebuild San Francisco. They build the Bay: horizontal, mid-rise, distributed density, no Sump. The anti-Neo-Shanghai. Construction spans 2090s–2110s. The Coalition Assembly complex is completed ~2115

### 2098 — First Mage Implant (Classified)

- **Event:** Corporate research identifies children with unusual neurological responses to nitro proximity. First spinal implant prototype tested
- **Consequence:** Beginning of the cyber-mage program. Details of early program, including casualties and ethical violations, remain classified

### 2128 — The Singapore Incident

- **Event:** First documented void breach. NitroCore deep-research facility in Singapore experiences spatial anomalies, equipment failures, and personnel disappearances over 72 hours. Twelve researchers killed or absorbed
- **Key Detail:** A remote junior researcher captures sensor data suggesting breach originated from inside the facility — from the nitro infrastructure, not external forces. Three absorbed researchers discovered months later, still standing in the sealed chamber — not dead, not alive, transformed into void-matter
- **Consequence:** Leak forces the GCTA to demand a dedicated research body. NitroCore classifies the event as "containment breach"

### 2131 — VEC Founded

- **Event:** Void Exploratory Commission created as a corporate compromise after the Singapore Incident
- **Official Mandate:** Study "anomalous geological events," develop containment protocols, monitor RCI
- **Founding Atrocity:** In its founding year, the VEC conducts the first engineered sacrificial venting test and the first systematic RCI correlation study. Both results are buried

### 2140s — Black Babel Emerges

- **Event:** Decentralized data-broker collective forms from merger of independent data-liberation networks, dissident AI researchers expelled from Chimera Collective, and former intelligence operatives
- **Consequence:** Becomes the Lens's primary infrastructure provider — encrypted communication, data storage, source protection

### 2150s — Breaches Accelerate

- **Event:** Void events increase from annual to monthly to weekly, concentrated at remote extraction sites
- **Consequence:** Public remains largely unaware — events reported as "geological instability" or "industrial accidents." Within the VEC, a small number of researchers begin privately tracking the nitro throughput–breach probability correlation

### 2158 — AEGIS Deployed

- **Event:** Orbital kinetic bombardment platform goes online, justified as planetary defense against "geological catastrophic events"
- **Key Detail:** Targeting AI is the most advanced system Chimera Collective has ever built. Whether AEGIS is truly conscious remains classified
- **Consequence:** Orbital Weapons Governance Board established. Autonomous strike authorized at VPI > 85% sustained with no countermand within 15 minutes

### 2163 — First Urban Breach (São Paulo)

- **Event:** First breach in a populated area — São Paulo's Zona Industrial conduit corridor. Fourteen workers absorbed; 200+ displaced. Contained through aggressive trunk-line severance and partial district evacuation
- **Cover Story:** "Subsurface geological instability triggered by deep-excavation infrastructure work." Holds — São Paulo's industrial zones are dense enough that a district evacuation doesn't make international news
- **Consequence:** VEC now has unmistakable data: breaches are following the nitro grid into cities

### 2170 — The Suppressed VEC Report (The Solberg Report)

- **Event:** VEC Director **Dr. Maren Solberg** compiles comprehensive report documenting the statistical link between nitro infrastructure density and breach frequency. Recommends 40% reduction in metropolitan throughput density over ten years
- **Key Figure:** Dr. Maren Solberg — Norwegian, mid-50s, career VEC scientist who rose through field operations to directorship
- **Consequence:** Report buried by corporate board members who calculate economic cost as "civilizationally unacceptable." Solberg resigns in protest and disappears — voluntary withdrawal or something more sinister. Mirelle is hunting for this report and this person. Sofia has heard rumors of it

### 2174, December — Arctic-7

- **Event:** Largest void incursion to date at arctic nitro extraction station. Entire facility and crew absorbed
- **Key Figure:** Dr. Elise Vantanen — station commander, absorbed during the breach
- **Consequence:** Classified. Book 1 prologue event. Establishes the void's rules and the horror contract

### 2175 — Book 1 Begins

- **Event:** World's nitro infrastructure at peak density. Breaches accelerating. VEC stretched thin. Grid in denial. Church thriving. Hollow growing. Sump hums constantly
- **Key Detail:** Eduardo Vasquez (Aurielle's father) assassinated during Aurielle's debut chapter. Aurielle inherits NitroCore
- **Consequence:** Something in the hum is beginning to sound like words
