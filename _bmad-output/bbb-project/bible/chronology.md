# Chronology

> Living Narrative Bible — Temporal Dimension
> Day-by-day tracking of story events

---

## Usage Instructions

This bible dimension tracks the **temporal progression** of the story:

- **Each day** is documented with its events by period
- **Available Periods:** Morning, Noon, Afternoon, Evening, Night
- **Each event** includes: description, characters, location, consequences

### Entry Format

```markdown
### Day [N]

**[Period]:** [Event Title]
- Details: [Specific description]
- Characters Involved: [Names]
- Location: [Place]
- Consequences: [Immediate results]
```

### Coherence Checks

Before adding an event, verify:
- [ ] Event occurs after its causes
- [ ] Travel times are realistic
- [ ] Characters are not in two places simultaneously
- [ ] Weather/season is consistent (if applicable)
