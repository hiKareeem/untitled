- **Fix options:** (A) Remove implant from Ch 9, use handheld camera consistently. (B) Update Ch 2 to mention the implant exists but she uses the handheld for different purposes (stills, different resolution, backup). (C) Establish in Ch 2 that the implant exists but she uses dedicated hardware for evidentiary-grade footage.

#### CRITICAL: Fuxi Timeline (Ch 4 vs Ch 8)

- **Ch 8:** Fuxi reflects the Monitor 6 spike was "three weeks ago." Nuwa says "one more week on the hold." Fuxi tells Nuwa "We're two months into the savings plan."

- **Contradiction 2:** "Two months into the savings plan" cannot be true if only 3 weeks have passed since the plan was scoped as "three months total."
- **Fix options:** (A) Change Ch 8's "three weeks ago" to ~5 days, and "two months" to "a week." (B) Adjust Ch 4 to imply they'd been saving before finding the specific P-3. (C) Add a time-jump indicator and adjust the hold to a longer negotiated period.
