### Timeline Analysis (⚠️ Critical)

**The issue:** Ch 16 states the first spike was "eleven weeks and four days ago" and the log was created "eleven weeks ago." This first spike matches the Ch 4 event (monitor 6, 31.2, ~40-second linear descent, Chen Wei's dismissal). However:

- **Ch 8 places Ch 4 at ~3 weeks prior** ("Spike from three weeks ago not recurred")
- **Ch 8 states savings are "two months in, four more months to P-3"**
- **Ch 16 states the vendor held the P-3 for "Five months"** (from Ch 4 discovery)
- **Therefore Ch 4 → Ch 16 ≈ 5 months ≈ 22 weeks**

But Ch 16 says the Ch 4 spike was **11 weeks** ago. Discrepancy: ~22 weeks vs ~11 weeks.

Additionally: "Eleven weeks. Fifty-three entries" at daily shift updates (5-7/week) = ~53-77 shifts. Calibrated to 11 weeks. If expanded to 22 weeks, entry count would need to be ~95-110+.

Also affected: "five months of chemical solvent exposure" (Nuwa's violet staining) — ties to same timeline.

**Resolution options (for author):**
- **A) Expand "eleven weeks" → ~20 weeks.** Adjust entry count (53→~95), spike intervals, "sitting on the blue textile eleven weeks ago." Most disruptive to prose rhythm.
- **B) Contract "Five months" → ~3 months.** Adjust vendor holding period, Nuwa's savings timeline. Tensions with Ch 8's "four more months."
- **C) Decouple the Ch 16 first spike from the Ch 4 spike.** Say the Ch 4 spike was earlier, and the "first" spike in Ch 16's counting is a later one that made Fuxi retroactively reframe Ch 4's event. Requires minor prose adjustment.
- **D) Accept as character approximation.** Fuxi's "eleven weeks" is imprecise memory. Less satisfying given his data-oriented characterization.

**Recommendation:** Option C is cleanest — minimal prose disruption. Remove the specific matching details (31.2, forty-two seconds, Chen Wei) from the "first spike" description, making it a different, later spike that prompted the log. The Ch 4 spike then becomes a precursor that Fuxi didn't fully register at the time.

**Resolution:** Option C applied per author approval. The Ch 4 spike is now referenced as "an earlier spike, months back, the one Chen Wei had dismissed as a fluctuation" — a precursor that "lodged in his body like a splinter." The first logged spike is 30.8/26s, second 31.2/22s. The forty-second descent on L103 correctly references the Ch 4 precursor, maintaining the 40→19 acceleration arc.
