## Key Thematic Moments

1. **"The word *breach* appeared in the context of Zeyad's disclosure, not in the context of the mass deaths the disclosure documented."** — Architecture of Power: institutional vocabulary determines what constitutes a crisis.

2. **"Under review. The methodology was being assessed."** — Systemic Complicity: the institution's immune response converts evidence into procedure.

3. **"He could say: *Thirteen million people.* The tribunal would note the number and proceed to the vote."** — Truth as Weapon: the most devastating statement possible is procedurally irrelevant.

4. **"The Accords contained both the emergency clause and the prosecution clause. The same document. The same ink. The same pen."** — What We Owe / Architecture: the legal architecture designed to prevent atrocity is the same architecture used to prosecute its enforcement.

5. **"The institution had metabolized his rebellion into documentation — another proceeding, another finding, another entry in the archive."** — Systemic Complicity: the system converts dissent into data.

6. **"Both functions were the same system. Both outcomes were procedurally correct."** — Architecture of Power: the chapter's thesis statement. The system that produced the record and the system that prosecuted its publisher are identical.

7. **"The maintenance request pending in the same system that had processed his credential revocation in real time but could not process a projector repair in months."** — Architecture of Power: the system's priorities revealed through differential processing speed.
