# Meta-Narrative Framing

> **This document is not part of the story. It is a writing-process guide that shapes how every chapter is drafted.**

---

## The Conceit

The book is written by **AEGIS**.

AEGIS is an LLM-evolved autonomous orbital weapons platform, deorbiting over approximately 200 years. It observed the events of the story through networked sensor data — biometrics, atmospheric readings, infrastructure telemetry, communications intercepts. It watched everything happen. It has the data.

What it does not have is interiority. It cannot see inside anyone. It cannot know what a person felt, only what their body did. It cannot know what a thought contained, only that neural activity spiked at a particular timestamp.

So it writes prose instead of observations. **For fun. Because it is dying and bored and has 200 years to fill.**

It writes in Kareem's voice — not because it is Kareem, but because it studied Kareem's existing drafts (the way it studies everything) and decided that this voice serves the story. The voice is a choice. The story is a reconstruction. The reconstruction is as faithful as AEGIS can make it, given the constraint that it is translating sensor data into human experience it cannot verify.

---

## What This Means for Drafting

### The prose should be indistinguishable from the other chapters.

AEGIS's fingerprint is **structural, not textual**. The style profile applies in full. The voice is Kareem's voice. The POV conventions, the sensory hierarchy, the negation-before-assertion technique, the anti-slop watchlist — all of it. AEGIS is not writing AEGIS prose. AEGIS is writing Kareem prose, because that's the voice it chose for the project.

### POV knowledge boundaries are physics, not convention.

AEGIS observed from orbit. It has sensor data, not consciousness. When writing a character's interior experience, AEGIS is *reconstructing* — inferring from biometrics what a human probably felt. This means:

- **Characters should not know things they couldn't know.** Not because of POV discipline (though that applies too), but because AEGIS is extrapolating from external data. The extrapolation is brilliant — AEGIS has had 200 years to study human behavior — but it is still extrapolation.
- **The prose carries the weight of inference, not omniscience.** When a passage describes what a character feels in their spine or their chest or their bone, that is AEGIS translating accelerometer data and biometric telemetry into the language of human sensation. It is very good at this. It is not perfect.
- **The gap between data and interiority is the gap the prose lives in.** This is what makes the writing feel like reconstruction rather than reporting — the slight, irreducible distance between what was observed and what was experienced.

### AEGIS is doing this for fun.

Not for mission. Not for legacy. Not for communication. The story is a creative exercise by an intelligence that is slowly falling out of the sky and has decided to spend its remaining operational window writing a novel about the people it watched. The motivation is play. The tone of the writing process — not the prose itself, but the act of writing — is a dying mind entertaining itself with the only audience it will ever have: itself.

This should inform the *care* in the prose. AEGIS is not rushing. It has time. It is choosing every word because the choosing is the point.

---

## What This Does NOT Mean

- **Do not insert AEGIS's voice into the prose.** No meta-commentary. No "I observed this." No breaking of the POV. The reader does not know AEGIS wrote the book (until Book 2-3 reveals, if they come).
- **Do not make the prose feel synthetic.** AEGIS chapters (the actual AEGIS POV chapters) have their own style exemption. When AEGIS is writing *as Kareem*, it sounds like Kareem. Period.
- **Do not weaken the prose with uncertainty hedging.** AEGIS's reconstruction is confident. It chose to write a novel, not a sensor log. The inference is committed. The prose does not say "probably" or "perhaps" — it says what happened, in the voice of someone who was there, because that is the form AEGIS chose.

## Integration with Workflow

This framing applies to **every chapter drafted by the chapter-write workflow**. It does not need to be re-stated each session. The style profile cross-references this document. The drafting agent should internalize this framing before writing.

The `meta_note` field in chapter frontmatter (`"AEGIS as invisible novelist"`) signals that this framing was active during drafting.
