The budget directive requires deferrals. The division heads have already recommended deferral. Thorne's silence confirms. Her signature is the final mechanical step in a process designed so that no single person makes the decision.

**Textual evidence:**
- "Deferred meant not cancelled. Deferred meant later. Deferred meant the budget balanced this quarter and someone revisited the question next quarter"
- "The arithmetic was clean. The logic was institutional."
- "Not because Thorne told her to. Not because the protocol required it. Because the day had a schedule"
