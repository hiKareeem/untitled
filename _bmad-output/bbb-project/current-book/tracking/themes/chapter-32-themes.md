## Detailed Thematic Analysis

### Systemic Complicity (Dominant)

The chapter's central thematic engine. Complicity escalates across three registers:

1. **Institutional complicity**: Transit junctions sealed before evacuation authorized. Containment protocol treats Sector 7 as expendable. "Will not be reinforced" = institutional abandonment.
2. **Operational complicity**: "Hold the perimeter" means "ensure no one escapes while the lattice absorbs them." The perimeter is not defensive — it's containment of the victims, not the threat.
3. **Documentary complicity**: After-action report falsified. "Anomalous observations: none" erases routing evidence. The field log records truth but cannot act on it.

**Key moment**: "The protocol became complicity" — the chapter's thesis statement, arriving in the garrison scene as Nikolai processes what he participated in.

**Escalation from previous chapters**:
- Ch 11: Passive omission (doesn't mention Deng's name in the hum)
- Ch 20: Active suppression (writes "No anomalous observations" knowing it's false)
- Ch 32: Falsification (writes routing observation, reads it, deletes it, writes "none")

### Architecture of Power (Dominant)

The void's intelligence is revealed through infrastructure behavior — it navigates the conduit network the way power navigates institutions: along established paths, toward concentrations of people, bypassing dead ends.

**Parallel structures**:
- Void routes through trunk-lines → Command routes through protocol
- Void bypasses dead-end corridors → Institution bypasses moral questions
- Void targets high-throughput junctions → Protocol targets high-population sectors

**The infrastructure itself is the weapon**: transit junctions sealed, perimeter enforced, containment zone established — all using the same infrastructure the void uses to reach its targets.

### Truth as Weapon (Strong)

The dual-record system reaches maximum strain. For the first time, Nikolai writes the truth in the after-action report (the routing observation paragraph), reads it, and *deletes it*. Previous chapters: he simply didn't write it. Ch 32: he writes it, confronts it, and destroys it. The deletion is more damning than the omission.

The field log records everything — including Jarek's observation, Jarek's question, and the protocol's non-answer. "Kowalski asked: 'What are we doing?' I said: 'Following the protocol.' The answer was not an answer."

### Progress Requires Atrocity (Strong)

"Well done" — Command's response to a perimeter that held while six thousand people were absorbed. The atrocity is the success. The protocol worked. The system functioned as designed.

"The containment zone will not be reinforced" — the sector is expendable. Not a failure of the system but a feature. The system decides which populations are worth protecting and which are acceptable losses.
