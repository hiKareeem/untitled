## Character: Dr. Elise Vantanen

### Emotional Arc Summary

Vantanen's arc compresses an entire emotional journey into a single prologue. She begins in professional unease — the hum has changed and she doesn't believe the institutional explanation. Through escalating scientific alarm and controlled dread, she pivots to defiant protectiveness, gaming the system to preserve the space for understanding. Alone in the monitoring bay, her vigilance shades into fascination as the impossible manifests, and fascination gives way to ontological surrender as the void's proposition overwhelms individual cognition. She does not choose to reach through the glass. Her arm moves "with the slow deliberation of someone reaching for a door they'd been walking toward for a very long time without knowing it." She is included, not consumed — and the distinction is the last thing her individual consciousness fails to communicate.

### Dominant Emotions

- **Primary**: Scientific fascination — the drive to understand overrides survival instinct
- **Secondary**: Professional protectiveness — shields her people and her autonomy from institutional machinery
- **Conflict**: Curiosity as ego vs. curiosity as vulnerability. "Being the discoverer means being safe from the discovery" — The Lie, tested and broken.

### Emotional State by Phase

- **Opening**: Professional unease masked by administrative compliance — signs off on tinnitus explanation she doesn't believe
- **Rising Action**: Controlled dread escalating through impossible data, institutional gaming, solitary vigilance
- **Midpoint**: Defiant protectiveness — "I'm asking you to think out loud before the system tells us to stop thinking"
- **Climax**: Fascination overriding survival — steps toward the crack; the void's proposition
- **Resolution**: Ontological surrender — included, not consumed. The distinction matters. She cannot communicate it.
