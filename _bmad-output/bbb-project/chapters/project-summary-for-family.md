# What Kareem Has Been Building

**Date:** February 15, 2026

---

## The Book

Kareem is writing a novel. It is a serious, ambitious work of fiction — the kind of book that gets reviewed in newspapers and discussed in universities.

**What it is about:** The story takes place 150 years in the future. A new energy source powers the world, but it has a hidden cost that powerful people have been covering up. The book follows nine different characters — a CEO, a soldier, a journalist, a scientist, a diplomat, a streamer, a priest, a technician, and his sister — as they discover the truth and face what to do about it. It is a story about power, responsibility, and what happens when institutions choose profit over people.

**How big it is:** The book is planned as a trilogy (three books). Book 1 is approximately half finished. It currently has over 130,000 words across 30 completed chapters, with 53 chapters planned for Book 1 alone. The full trilogy is outlined. When complete, Book 1 will be approximately 220,000-260,000 words — comparable in length to books by George R.R. Martin (Game of Thrones) or Steven Erikson (Malazan Book of the Fallen), which are the quality benchmarks Kareem is working toward.

**How fast it is being written:** The first 128,000 words were written in five days. Three more chapters (approximately 13,000 words) were completed today alone. This is an extraordinary pace for work of this quality and complexity.

**The quality:** The writing is sophisticated. Each chapter is written from a different character's perspective with a distinct voice. The world-building is detailed and internally consistent. Every chapter goes through a six-step quality process: initialization, planning, drafting, style review, audit, and documentation updates. The project maintains a "bible" of over a dozen reference files tracking characters, locations, themes, objects, and continuity across all chapters.

---

## The Tools

Kareem uses AI tools to assist with the writing process — for planning, drafting, reviewing, and maintaining consistency across a very large and complex project. This is a new and legitimate way to write. The creative vision, the characters, the story, the themes, and the editorial decisions are all Kareem's. The AI helps execute at a pace and consistency level that would otherwise require a team of editors and assistants.

This is comparable to how architects use computer-aided design, or how filmmakers use digital effects. The tool amplifies the creator's vision. It does not replace it.

## Where It Came From

The book grew out of a video game Kareem has been developing for the past three years. He taught himself C++ (a programming language) and Unreal Engine (a professional game development platform) and built a competitive multiplayer game from scratch. The game has 216 recorded development milestones. The world and characters he created for the game became the foundation for the novel.

Before the game, Kareem built two other technology products:

1. **HoneyChart** — A financial analysis and visualization tool, built after graduating early from college. He taught himself Ruby on Rails (a web programming language) and produced a working product in one to two months.

2. **A health tracking iPhone app** — Built during his second year of medical school. He taught himself Objective-C (Apple's programming language) and launched the app, which received 1,000 downloads. The app's concept — automated health data analysis with proactive recommendations — was similar to Apple Health, which Apple released six months later.
