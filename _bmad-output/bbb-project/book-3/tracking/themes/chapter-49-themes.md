She has not been consumed by the machine. She has not been corrupted by the position. The machine did not need to change her. The distinction had been absent from the beginning.

- "The hum did not distinguish between the wood and the skin."

Indistinguishable from the line items that described the grid's permanent function because the costs were the grid's permanent function."

The operator who sat at the desk since the gala is the operator who sits at the desk now. The same person. The same function. The machine did not change her because there was never a boundary to cross. The horror is not consumption. The horror is continuity.

**The Lie Broken:** "If you maintain your humanity, the institution cannot consume you." She maintained her humanity. The institution did not consume her. The institution did not need to consume her.
