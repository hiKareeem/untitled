## Epigraph Resonance

**Epigraph:** Osei-Mensah, K., *Disturbance Ecology: Succession and Equilibrium* (2148)

> "A climax community is the state an ecosystem reaches when its rate of disturbance and its rate of recovery have equalized. The community does not arrive at climax. It discovers, upon measurement, that it has been in climax for some time before the measurement was taken. The transition was not an event. The transition was the period during which the distinction between disturbance and recovery ceased to be measurable."

**Thematic mapping:**
- Climax community = Aurielle as operator-machine
- Rate of disturbance = crises (BLACKWEIR, SHEPHERD, Kindling, Proposition)
- Rate of recovery = institutional processing (quarterly cycle, competence, authorization)
- "Does not arrive at climax" = did not become the machine
- "Discovers, upon measurement" = the quarterly projection reveals what was already true
- "Transition was not an event" = no single moment of corruption or consumption
- "Distinction ceased to be measurable" = boundary between operator and machine never existed

**Assessment:** Oblique on first read. Resolves on reread. The ecology language (disturbance/recovery) maps to institutional language (crisis/management) without being on-the-nose. The reader who tracks Aurielle's arc will recognize: the quarterly projection is the measurement that reveals she has been in climax (operator = machine) for some time before the measurement was taken. The transition was the 40 chapters during which the distinction between Aurielle and the grid ceased to be measurable.
