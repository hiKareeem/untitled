## Style Audit

### Voice Metrics

**Sentence length:**
- Average: ~22 words (within profile: 15-25)
- Bimodal distribution maintained: dense operational passages (40-60 words) alternating with single-sentence paragraphs (4-8 words)
- Shortest: "She was the response." (4 words)
- Longest: The alert cascade paragraph (~85 words) — functional density, enacts simultaneity

**Paragraph strategy:**
- Single-sentence paragraphs: 18 instances
- Used for emphasis ("The model worked."), punctuation ("This was not singular."), and structural beats ("She opened the next report.")
- Dense processing blocks: alert sequence, manual override, recovery curve
- Rhythm: operational urgency conveyed through data accumulation, not prose intensity

**Paratactic structure:**
- Dominant throughout
- Compound sentences used for institutional processing (the crisis summary description, the recovery subroutine explanation)
- Coordination preferred over subordination
- "And" used sparingly for emphasis ("And the grid had recovered.")

- No second instance — clean

**Sensory hierarchy:**

- Visual: secondary (display wall, throughput numbers, facility-level interface)
- Tactile: minimal (walnut surface in closing section only)
- Temperature: absent (maintained from B3C1/C9)
- Olfactory/gustatory: absent

**Emotion delivery:**
- No named emotions ✓
- Weight conveyed through operational processing, structural absence, data accumulation
- The horror is in the return to baseline, not in the disruption

**Technical vocabulary:**
- Used without explanation: throughput, redistribution algorithms, tolerance band, manual override, capacity margins, rerouting pathways
- Institutional register maintained throughout
- No vocabulary breaks

## Thematic Audit

### Dominant Theme: Architecture of Power

**Deployment:**
- The grid's structure absorbs resistance
- Twelve simultaneous strikes overwhelm redistribution capacity temporarily
- Emergency protocols restore baseline automatically
- The grid recovers not through adaptation but through return to designed state
- Aurielle's competence is the grid's competence — she IS the architecture

**Evidence:**
- Manual override: the infrastructure's last-resort architecture
- Recovery subroutine: automated restoration, no human decision required
- Eduardo's engineering: the tolerance band, redistribution algorithms designed for recovery
- The grid defeats the model by recovering, not by learning

**Effectiveness:** Strong. The chapter demonstrates that the grid's power is not in its resistance to disruption but in its capacity to absorb disruption and return to baseline. The architecture is the power.

### Strong Theme: Systemic Complicity

**Deployment:**
- Aurielle's competence prevents the model from working at scale
- The thing she does well (operational excellence) is the thing that defeats the model
- The manual override saves the grid and defeats the throughput reduction
- Three of twelve struck facilities are on Thorne's voluntary reduction list — sabotage and strategy converge
- The crisis summary will describe operational excellence, not the model's validation

**Evidence:**
- "The machine's most competent operator, executing competently, is the thing that prevents the model from working at scale."
- She does not open the appendix (the data proving the model)
- She opens the next report (the machine's heartbeat)
- The institutional vocabulary will convert proof into narrative about resilience

**Effectiveness:** Strong. The chapter's horror is that competence is complicity. Aurielle holds the grid because she is competent. The grid defeats the model because she defeats it. The system preserves itself through the excellence of its operators.

### Ambient Theme: The Cost of Knowing

**Deployment:**
- She hears the hum change (the model working in real-time)
- She pulls the Anchor Zone data (coherence reduced, proportional, clean)
- She knows the model works
- She does not open the appendix
- She opens the next report

**Evidence:**
- "The model worked and the grid held." — both facts are true
- The appendix contains the six-hour window data
- The data is there, she knows it is there, she does not look at it
- The closing pair: negation (appendix unopened) + echo (next report opened)

**Effectiveness:** Ambient but present. The cost of knowing is not the knowledge itself but what the knowledge does not produce. She knows the model works. The knowledge produces no institutional action. The knowledge is filed. The filing IS the action.

### Thematic Coherence

The three themes work in concert:
- **Architecture of Power:** the grid's structure enables recovery
- **Systemic Complicity:** the operator's competence executes the recovery
- **Cost of Knowing:** the operator knows what the recovery defeats and continues

The chapter's thesis: the model works, the grid recovers, competence is the mechanism of both, and the institutional register processes all three facts identically.
