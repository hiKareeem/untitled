## Epigraph Analysis

**Source:** *Testimony of the Antenna*, Kira (unauthorized recordings), Fragment 14

**Text:** *"You can hear me adjusting the scale. That's the sound. That clicking — that's the tare. I'm precise about this. I want you to know I'm precise about this. The number is the number. The number is not the problem."*

**First-read interpretation:** Mage documenting her pharmaceutical process. Precision as professional practice.

**Post-chapter interpretation:** Precision is defense mechanism. "The number is the number. The number is not the problem." = denial. The number is exactly the problem. Her insistence that it isn't is the drift speaking.

**Structural irony:** Kira's own words, recorded without her editorial control, used as epigraph for a chapter where she cannot see herself clearly. The testimony she gave is more honest than the testimony she's living. The unauthorized recording captures what the authorized narrator cannot.

**Oblique thematic framing:** The chapter is about a woman who measures everything (milligram scale, neural coherence data, viewer counts, eleven-second delays) and cannot measure the thing that matters (her own cognitive state). The epigraph establishes this before the chapter begins.
