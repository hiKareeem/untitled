## 4. THEMATIC AUDIT

### ✓ 4.1 Dominant theme: Moral Obligation to Consequences
**Status:** STRONG  
**Evidence:** Chapter structured as validation + casualties arriving simultaneously. Sofia published the model (fulfilled obligation to study the crisis). Kindling executed it (she did not coordinate). The casualties are hers anyway. Authorship = moral weight regardless of agency. "She had known what the model would do to these populations if it worked at global scale. She had published the knowledge." (line 141) The obligation was necessary and insufficient. The model worked. People are dying because the model worked.

### ✓ 4.2 Secondary theme: Truth as Weapon (evolved)
**Status:** STRONG  
**Evidence:** The weapon validated at planetary scale. Sofia's model was truth — scientifically rigorous, methodologically complete, empirically verified. The Kindling used the truth as a map. The chokepoints (page 43, Figure 7) became targets. The dependency chains (page 51, Table 3) became casualty predictions. Truth was necessary. Truth was weaponized. Correctness produces the horror. "The model was correct about both simultaneously." (line 139)

### ✓ 4.3 Tertiary theme: Systemic Complicity
**Status:** STRONG  
**Evidence:** Infrastructure that produces breach expansion produces potable water. Model that reduces one reduces the other. "The conduit network carried nitro to the Anchor Zones and nitro to the desalination plants through the same trunk lines. Reduce the trunk line, reduce both." (line 139) No position outside the system. No way to save one population without harming another. The distinction between atmospheric science and humanitarian impact doesn't exist in the infrastructure.

### ✓ 4.4 Quaternary theme: What We Owe
**Status:** MODERATE-STRONG  
**Evidence:** Sofia fulfilled the obligation — studied the crisis, found the signal, mapped the infrastructure, published the model. The model worked. The world is not healed. "The obligation was necessary and insufficient" (implied throughout). Elena parallel: both scientists fulfilled obligations, both lost control of their work, both discovered the work becomes the system's work regardless of intentions.

### ✓ 4.5 Water motif (oblique)
**Status:** PRESENT  
**Evidence:** Sofia drinks tap water from municipal reservoir (Sierra snowmelt, legacy system, not grid-dependent) while 4M people in Gulf coast have no water. The parallel is unnarrated — reader makes the connection. Water's availability is "a function of geography and infrastructure history" (line 153). Four million people do not have this water (line 155). The model works. The wells fail.
