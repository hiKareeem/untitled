Lines ~83-101, ~111-127, ~145-161 — Sustained institutional analysis passages run 300-400 words each. The mandate parsing, compendium description, and Working Group finding sections are dense with procedural vocabulary. This is inherent to Zeyad's register and the chapter's thesis (institutional machinery processing), but the cumulative weight may approach fortissimo. The chapter has minimal environmental variation to offset this density.

**Flag 2 (Delegate Language Proximity):**  
Line 185 — Pacific Rim delegate uses "replacement framework" and "adjustment of parameters rather than negotiation of terms." Narration clarifies she didn't read Zeyad's document and reached the conclusion independently through institutional vocabulary. The proximity is deliberate (seeds Ch42 co-option), but the phrasing is very close to what Zeyad would write. Consider whether the convergence reads as institutional architecture (intended) or as POV bleed (unintended).

**Flag 3 (Pen Stillness Register):**  
The pen's stillness is the chapter's emotional register — more devastating than being ignored because the institution arrives at his conclusions without needing him. This works thematically but means the chapter has limited physical variation. Zeyad sits, watches, walks home. The stillness is the point, but it may read as static across 4,600 words. The corridor/departure scene provides some movement, but the bulk is gallery observation.
