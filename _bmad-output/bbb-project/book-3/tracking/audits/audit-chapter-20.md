### The Section Break as Structural Gap

**Between sections 3 and 4:** The chapter's most important structural decision.

The eleven minutes of Kira standing silent at the junction do not exist in Kira's POV because Kira was not present. The section break = the gap. The reader jumps from Nuwa moving to the white ceiling the way Kira jumps from the step-function to the white ceiling. The structural absence mirrors the experiential absence.

AEGIS, reconstructing from the stream recording, would have access to the eleven minutes — but AEGIS is writing in Kira's voice, and Kira's voice was not present. The meta-narrative conceit reinforces the structural choice: the gap in the prose is an honest gap, not a narrative omission.

**Executed:** ✓

### Mode Transition: TEXTURE → FRACTURE

**TEXTURE (sections 1-2):** Quiet register, professional setup, stream-voice narrating, data accumulating. The tension builds through numbers, not through prose intensity.

**FRACTURE (section 3):** Prose fractures. Short declaratives. Fragments. "Coherence: 0.97." / "Thirty seconds." / "Then nothing." The structure breaks as Kira's interiority breaks.

**Clinical (section 4):** Not TEXTURE, not FRACTURE — a third register. The filing system processing what it cannot process. The prose is organized, competent, the filing system's architecture applied to the gap. But the organization is hollow. The gap is categorized but not filled.

**Transition earned:** ✓
