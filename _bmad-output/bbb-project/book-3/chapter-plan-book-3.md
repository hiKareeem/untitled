## Story Concept

### Logline

Six months after the Broadcast, the throughput reduction works — the void stops expanding, the boundary stabilizes, and the world discovers that stopping extinction is not the same as fixing anything.

### Premise

The void does not consume the planet. The Kindling strikes succeed — Sofia's model validated at scale. Breach expansion slows, then stops. But dependent regions collapse. The cure has a body count. The absorbed do not come back. The boundary is permanent. The aggregate formulates the Proposition: equilibrium confirmed, boundary permanent, physics not invitation. Nobody gets resolution.

### Tone

Bakker more than Erikson or GRRM. The horror of intelligence. Being correct does not save you. Moral injury rendered as ontological weight.

### Key Principles

1. **No re-exposition** — Chapter 1 drops into post-Broadcast worldstate
2. **The Proposition as convergence** — Phase 3-4 boundary, all POVs register it in 1-2 chapter window
3. **Selective "continuation is—" structure** — choices get endings; institutional paralysis gets the dash
4. **AEGIS as meta-narrator** — internal framing device; reader never learns AEGIS wrote the book
5. **Character-forward arcs** — each arc framed as an "inevitable question" derived from the character's nature

### The Inevitable Questions

| POV | Question | Answer |
|-----|----------|--------|
| **Aurielle** | What happens when you remove the enabler and the machine still runs? | She IS the machine. There was never a boundary. |
| **Nephthys** | What happens when your theology is validated and it doesn't mean what you needed? | Biology takes her before the Proposition. She was right and never finds out. |
| **Malachi** | What happens when the institutional man finally confesses? | The institution absorbs the confession as history, not imperative. |
| **Sofia** | What happens when the correct model is validated at scale? | Pyrrhic. The cure has a body count. Correct and insufficient. |
| **Kira** | What happens when the antenna has to choose between the signal and herself? | She goes back to the Academy — the institution that broke her — to be fixed. She chooses herself. |
| **Nikolai** | What happens when the soldier's perimeter becomes a door and people use it? | The Threshold volunteers break him. He finds the discipline of honest observation. |
| **Zeyad** | What happens when the institution adopts your ideas without your name? | Co-option without attribution. The subtlest institutional violence. |
| **Nuwa** | What happens when the invisible woman becomes visible? | Visibility makes her a target and a resource. She becomes what institutions couldn't: someone who counts. |
| **AEGIS** | What kills the ultimate weapon when extinction is off the table? | The throughput reduction that saves the world starves its power. It spends its remaining energy writing. |
