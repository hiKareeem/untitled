# Lexicon — Standardized Terminology & Character Registers

> Writing reference for consistent language across the trilogy.
> 150 years of lexical drift from 2025 to 2175 — not alien-speak, but organic evolution.

---

## 1. Church of the Blessed Nitro (The Flame)

> Catholic-modeled. Familiar, ritualistic, sincere, uncomfortable.

### Hierarchy

| Title | Catholic Parallel | Role |
|-------|-------------------|------|
| **The Luminarch** | Pope | Head of the global Church. Resides in New Geneva. |
| **Arch-Prelate** | Cardinal/Archbishop | Regional authority. Malachi holds the European See. |
| **Prelate** | Priest | Local parish leader. Conducts the Radiance. |
| **Acolyte** | Deacon | Junior clergy. |
| **The Devoted** | Laity | The faithful. |
| **The Illuminated** | Saints | Canonized martyrs of the nitro age. |

### Core Tenets (The Five Harmonies)

1. **The Hum is the Voice of Creation.** To hear the hum is to hear God.
2. **The Silence Before was humanity's fall.** Pre-nitro darkness was our fallen state.
3. **Provision is proof of love.** Nitro's abundance is evidence of a benevolent cosmos.
4. **To serve the infrastructure is to serve the divine.** Conduit workers do holy work.
5. **The void is trial, not judgment.** Endurance through faith is the correct response.

### The Seven Resonances (Sacraments)

| Resonance | Catholic Parallel | Description |
|-----------|-------------------|-------------|
| **The Awakening** | Baptism | Infant exposed to hum in consecrated space. Drop of blessed nitro on forehead. *"You were in Silence. Now you hear."* |
| **The Attunement** | Confirmation | Adolescent ceremony. Extended hum exposure. Taught to harmonize. |
| **The Anointing** | Eucharist | Weekly. Blessed nitro to forehead during the Radiance. The hum intensifies. They call it "hearing the Voice." It is suffering. They think it is God. |
| **The Unburdening** | Confession | Confess in a booth with nitro-infused walls. The hum is louder. The Voice absorbs your sins. |
| **The Binding** | Marriage | Partners anointed together. Their hums "join." |
| **The Calling** | Holy Orders | Ordination. 24-hour nitro exposure vigil in a resonance chamber. |
| **The Returning** | Last Rites | Anointing of the dying. *"Return to the Voice. You were never separate."* |

### Liturgical Calendar

| Observance | Parallel | Description |
|------------|----------|-------------|
| **Cascade Remembrance** | Lent | Mourning the Silence Before. Fasting, gratitude for deliverance. |
| **First Light** | Easter/Christmas | Holiest day. Celebrates first nitro capture (2038). |
| **The Uplift** | Pentecost | Feast celebrating global nitro spread (2085). |
| **Breach Vigils** | Prayer vigils | After void events. Faithful harmonize to "strengthen the Voice." |

### The Radiance (Order of Service)

1. **Processional** — Prelate enters. Vestments of nitro-glass thread catch light.
2. **Opening** — *"We were in Silence. The Voice spoke. We heard."*
3. **First Reading** — From the Book of Radiance.
4. **Psalm of Resonance** — Congregation harmonizes with the hum.
5. **Second Reading** — Often from lives of the Illuminated.
6. **Homily** — Themes: gratitude, endurance, the Voice's constancy.
7. **The Anointing** — Blessed nitro to each forehead. *"Hear the Voice."*
8. **Communal Harmonization** — Full congregation matches the hum. The climax. Hundreds of voices merging with conduit resonance. Beautiful. Transcendent. Built on screams.
9. **Closing** — *"Go in Radiance. Carry the Voice. Fear not the Silence."*

### Nephthys's Counter-Liturgy

| Orthodox | Nephthys | Shift |
|----------|----------|-------|
| The Voice (singular) | **The Chorus** (plural) | She hears multiple voices — the absorbed are speaking |
| Resonance (alignment) | **Witness** (listening) | You don't align — you listen |
| The Silence Before (past) | **The Silence Ahead** (future) | The real silence is coming |
| The Anointing | Abolished | The hum is pain. You don't anoint with pain. |
| Harmonization | **Listening** | Silence together. Then speak what you heard. |
| The Radiance | **The Gathering** | No vestments, no ritual order. |

**Imani's role:** Institutional memory. Remembers the old forms. Worries about the new. *"People need structure, Nephthys. You can't just give them the void and tell them to listen."*

## 10. Character Speech Registers

### Aurielle Vasquez — Corporate → Corrupted

**Early:** Reform-speak. "We can do better." "There has to be another way." Active voice. Personal pronouns. She believes language can change reality.

**Late:** Thorne-speak. Passive voice. Institutional nouns. "The situation requires." "Operational continuity demands." The corruption is linguistic before it's moral — she starts talking like the Chair before she starts acting like it.

**Marker:** When Aurielle uses "we" meaning NitroCore instead of "they," the transition is underway.

### Mirelle Dubois — Surgical Precision

Sharp, economical, cutting. French-inflected English (occasional French when emotional or exhausted). Hates euphemism — she translates corporate language into plain speech as a reflex. Her notes vs. published pieces: notes are raw and angry; pieces are surgical.

**Marker:** Mirelle never uses corporate euphemisms without immediately translating them. *"Sump lifecycle management — that means they're deciding who dies."*

### Nephthys — Orthodox → Prophetic

**Early:** Church language. The Voice. Resonance. The Silence Before. Liturgical precision.

**Late:** Raw, unmediated. The Chorus. Witness. The language of direct experience, not scripture. She speaks in fragments when the chorus is loud.

**Marker:** When Nephthys stops capitalizing "the Voice" in her speech — when it becomes "the voice" or "the chorus" — she's left the Church behind.

### Zeyad Al-Fahim — Diplomatic → Broken

**Early:** Treaty language. "I must register my objection." "With respect to the precedent established." Formal, measured, multilingual undertones (Arabic rhythm under stress).

**Late:** The formal cage breaks. "You're going to let them die." Arabic escapes — not code-switching, but the language of genuine feeling slipping through diplomatic armor.

**Marker:** When Zeyad uses his grandfather's name in argument instead of "the precedent," he's fighting with his heart, not the Accords.

### Nikolai Volkov — Operational → Cracked

Clipped. Acronym-heavy. Designations instead of names. "Target," "sector," "contact." Russian syntax under extreme stress (article-dropping, inverted word order).

**Marker:** When Nikolai uses a person's NAME instead of a designation, something has broken through the operational layer. Jarek saying his name from inside the void is the crack.

### Fuxi Lin — Technical → Radicalized

**Early:** Corporate infrastructure vocabulary. "Conduit maintenance technician." "RCI reading within tolerance." "Scheduled inspection."

**Late:** Kindling language. "Sacrifice district." "The Grid's inventory." "Managed attrition."

**Marker:** Each new Kindling term replaces a corporate term. The shift is his radicalization made audible.

### Sofia Reyes — Hedged → Direct

**Early:** Scientific hedging. "The data suggests." "Further analysis may indicate." Passive voice. Qualifiers everywhere.

**Late:** "The data proves." "They know." Active voice. No qualifiers. She's no longer a VEC scientist — she's a witness.

**Marker:** Loss of hedging = gain of conviction.

### Kira — Performance → Raw

**Early:** Platform persona. Bright, branded, hyperconnected. Speaks in broadcast cadence — everything optimized for the Feed.

**Late:** Post-broadcast. Less filtered, more direct. The 11-second scream stripped the performance layer. She can't put it back.

**Marker:** When Kira speaks without checking how it sounds to an audience, she's changed.

### Nuwa Lin — Silence as Language

Speaks less than anyone. No jargon, no euphemism. Just what she sees. *"There was a shimmer. Then he wasn't there."*

Her silence is refusal — she will not perform legibility for systems that have written her off. When she speaks at length, it lands like a hammer.

**Marker:** Nuwa's longest speeches are her most important moments.
