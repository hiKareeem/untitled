# Custom Framework — Build Your Own Structure

**Best for:** Experimental fiction, literary fiction, non-linear narratives, experienced authors with specific visions

**Philosophy:** You know your story best. Build a structure that serves your unique vision.

## Common Custom Structures

### The Episodic Structure
- Series of relatively self-contained episodes
- Character or theme links them together
- Good for: Picaresque novels, journey stories, slice-of-life

### The Parallel Structure
- Two or more storylines running simultaneously
- May converge at the end
- Good for: Multi-generational sagas, ensemble casts

### The Frame Structure
- Story within a story
- Outer narrative frames inner narrative
- Good for: Metafiction, oral tradition stories

### The Reverse Chronology
- Story told backwards
- Effect before cause
- Good for: Mystery, trauma narratives

### The Mosaic Structure
- Multiple perspectives/fragments
- Reader assembles the whole
- Good for: Complex themes, multiple truths

---

## Validation Questions

Before finalizing your custom structure, ask:

1. Does every phase serve a clear purpose?
2. Is there progression (things change)?
3. Are the transitions between phases clear?
4. Can readers follow the structure?
5. Does the structure support your theme?
6. Does it feel right for THIS story?

---

Once you've answered the questions above, your custom framework becomes a phase structure:

```
PHASE 2: [Your name]
...
```
