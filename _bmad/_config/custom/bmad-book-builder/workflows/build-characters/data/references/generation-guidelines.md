# Character Generation Guidelines

This document provides comprehensive guidelines for autonomous character generation in Modes B and C.

## Core Generation Principles

### 1. Specificity Over Generality

**PRINCIPLE:** Every character must be unique and specific to their story context.

**Requirements:**
- Concrete details instead of placeholder text
- Specific experiences, not vague backgrounds
- Distinctive traits, not generic archetypes
- Unique voice patterns, not could-be-anyone speech

**Specificity Test:** Could I swap this character's name for another and have it still work? If yes, it's too generic.

**Examples:**

❌ **Generic:** "She's attractive and intelligent"
✅ **Specific:** "Her sharp eyes miss nothing, and she has a habit of tapping her forefinger when she's analyzing a problem"

❌ **Generic:** "He had a normal childhood"
✅ **Specific:** "He grew up above his father's bakery, waking at 4am to the smell of bread, learning to read from the discarded newspapers customers left behind"

---

### 2. Contradictions Over Consistency

**PRINCIPLE:** Characters are interesting because they're contradictory, not because they're consistent.

**Minimum Requirement:** 5 genuine contradictions per character (AgentAdam methodology)

**Types of Contradictions:**

1. **Values vs Actions** — What they believe vs what they do
2. **Self-Image vs Reality** — How they see themselves vs how they're perceived
3. **Conscious vs Unconscious** — What they think they want vs what they actually need
4. **Idealism vs Pragmatism** — Their principles vs their compromises
5. **Past vs Present** — Who they were vs who they're becoming
6. **Additional Tensions** — Any other opposing forces

**Contradiction Test:** Are there at least 5 genuine tensions? If the character feels consistent, you haven't gone deep enough.

---

### 3. Psychological Grounding

**PRINCIPLE:** Desires, fears, and contradictions must form a coherent psychological whole.

**Requirements:**
- Desires must make sense given background
- Fears must have understandable origins
- Contradictions must connect to core psychology
- Blind spots must be believable

**Psychology Test:** Do desires, fears, and contradictions form a coherent whole? Does the character make emotional sense?

**Framework:** Apply the 5-Phase Psychological Development Framework (see `character-frameworks.md`)

---

### 4. Narrative Functionality

**PRINCIPLE:** Every character must serve the story while being authentically themselves.

**Requirements:**
- Clear story role (protagonist, antagonist, mentor, etc.)
- Creates or embodies conflict/tension
- Arc is essential to story
- Relationships drive narrative

**Story Integration Test:** If I removed this character, would the story still work? If yes, their role isn't essential enough.

---

## Generation Quality Checklist

### Before Finalizing Any Generated Character:

✅ **Completeness:** All 9 sections populated with meaningful content
✅ **Specificity:** Content is unique to THIS character, not generic
✅ **Contradictions:** At least 5 genuine internal tensions
✅ **Psychology:** Desires, fears, contradictions form coherent whole
✅ **Voice:** Distinctive speech patterns, recognizable without tags
✅ **Arc:** Clear transformation with starting point, catalysts, ending
✅ **Consistency:** All sections align (voice matches psychology, etc.)
✅ **Story Integration:** Character serves clear story role
✅ **Emotional Truth:** Character feels authentic, not stereotypical

## Mode-Specific Guidelines

### Mode B: Autonomous Generation (Input-Based)

**Context:** Author provides concept, agent generates full character

**Guidelines:**
- Use author's input as foundation
- Expand intelligently but respect author's vision
- Fill gaps with specific, story-appropriate details
- Ensure all 9 sections are complete
- Apply 5-Phase Psychological Framework
- Create distinctive voice and mannerisms
- Design coherent transformation arc
- Make character specific, not generic

**Generation Process:**
1. Analyze author's concept for core elements
2. Check story bible for context and existing characters
3. Generate all 9 sections with specific content
4. Ensure internal consistency
5. Verify against quality checklist
6. Present for author review and refinement

---

### Mode C: Free Generation (No Initial Input)

**Context:** Agent generates character(s) from story context alone

**Guidelines:**
- Analyze story context to determine needed character types
- Ensure diversity across generated cast
- Apply 5-Phase Psychological Framework to each character
- Create distinctive voices and mannerisms for each
- Design coherent arcs that serve the story
- Avoid stereotypes and tokenism

**Single Character Generation:**
- Determine most useful character type for story
- Generate fully developed character
- Ensure they fill a story need

**Multiple Character Generation (3-5):**
- Use Party Mode for diverse perspectives
- Ensure variety in: gender, background, personality, voice, roles
- Include: protagonist-type, antagonist-type (or conflict-generator), supporting character(s)
- Design relationships and conflicts between characters
- Each character should be fully developed

**Diversity Requirements:**
- Different backgrounds and social origins
- Varied personality types
- Distinct voices and speech patterns
- Different roles and story functions
- Avoid token representation — each character must be fully developed

## Common Generation Pitfalls to Avoid

### ❌ Generic Descriptions

**Problem:** "He's tall and handsome"
**Solution:** "He's 6'2" with the stooped shoulders of someone who's spent too much time indoors, and a jawline that sharpens when he's frustrated"

### ❌ Placeholder Content

**Problem:** "To be determined" or generic backgrounds
**Solution:** Always generate specific content, even if author might change it later

### ❌ Insufficient Contradictions

**Problem:** Fewer than 5 genuine contradictions
**Solution:** Systematically apply all 6 contradiction types

### ❌ Generic Voice

**Problem:** Could be anyone speaking
**Solution:** Specific speech patterns, vocabulary, mannerisms, thought style

### ❌ Unclear Arc

**Problem:** Transformation is vague or unearned
**Solution:** Clear starting point, specific catalysts, earned transformation, distinct ending

### ❌ Stereotypes

**Problem:** Character is a flat archetype
**Solution:** Specific details, contradictions, and depth that subvert stereotypes

## Party Mode Integration (Mode C Multiple Character Generation)

When generating multiple characters in Mode C, use Party Mode to ensure diversity:

**Benefits:**
- Diverse perspectives on what characters could exist
- Varied voices and backgrounds
- Avoidance of single perspective's biases
- Richer, more unexpected character possibilities

**Process:**
1. Invoke Party Mode with detailed story requirements
2. Let multiple AI personas collaborate on character creation
3. Process output to ensure all quality standards met
4. Create individual dossier files for each character
5. Verify diversity and avoid stereotypes

---

## Post-Generation Review

After generating any character, ask:

1. **Specificity:** Is there anything generic that could be made more specific?
2. **Contradictions:** Are there at least 5 genuine tensions?
3. **Psychology:** Does everything connect logically?
4. **Voice:** Would I recognize this character without dialogue tags?
5. **Arc:** Is this the ONLY way this specific character could transform?
6. **Story Role:** Does this character serve a clear narrative purpose?

If any answer is "no," revise before presenting to author.
