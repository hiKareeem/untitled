# Save the Cat: Complete Framework Structure

## Framework Overview

**Name:** Save the Cat
**Type:** Beat-sheet structure
**Total Beats:** 15
**Acts:** 4
**Origin:** Blake Snyder

## Complete Beat Structure

### Act 1: Setup (Beats 1-5)

#### 1. Opening Image
- **Purpose:** Set tone and show protagonist's current state
- **Chapter Target:** 1
- **Key Elements:** Establish status quo, hint at transformation needed

#### 2. Theme Stated
- **Purpose:** Hint at the central theme
- **Chapter Target:** 1-2
- **Key Elements:** Often spoken by another character, subtle foreshadowing

#### 3. Setup
- **Purpose:** Introduce characters, stakes, and goal
- **Chapter Target:** 1-5
- **Key Elements:** Establish protagonist, show what's at stake, define world

#### 4. Catalyst
- **Purpose:** Inciting incident that disrupts status quo
- **Chapter Target:** 2-3
- **Key Elements:** Break into Act 1, story problem emerges

#### 5. Debate
- **Purpose:** Protagonist hesitates, makes decision
- **Chapter Target:** 3-5
- **Key Elements:** Fear and resistance, decision to accept the call

### Act 2: Response (Beats 6-8)

#### 6. Break into Two
- **Purpose:** Protagonist leaves comfort zone
- **Chapter Target:** 5-6
- **Key Elements:** Enter new world, commit to the journey

#### 7. B Story
- **Purpose:** Introduction of secondary storyline
- **Chapter Target:** 6-8
- **Key Elements:** Often romance or secondary character arc, carries theme

#### 8. Fun and Games
- **Purpose:** Exploring the new world, core story
- **Chapter Target:** 8-15
- **Key Elements:** Promise of the premise, central story conflict

### Act 3: Attack (Beats 9-12)

#### 9. Midpoint
- **Purpose:** False victory or defeat, stakes raised
- **Chapter Target:** 15-16
- **Key Elements:** Shift from reactive to active, raise stakes

#### 10. Bad Guys Close In
- **Purpose:** Pressure builds, obstacles mount
- **Chapter Target:** 16-18
- **Key Elements:** Internal and external conflicts intensify

#### 11. All Is Lost
- **Purpose:** Major defeat, seeming end
- **Chapter Target:** 18-19
- **Key Elements:** Whiff of death, lowest point

#### 12. Dark Night of the Soul
- **Purpose:** Moment of despair
- **Chapter Target:** 19-20
- **Key Elements:** Hope is lost, lesson from B Story applied

### Act 4: Resolution (Beats 13-15)

#### 13. Break into Three
- **Purpose:** Realization, new hope
- **Chapter Target:** 20-21
- **Key Elements:** Solution emerges, protagonist finds strength

#### 14. Finale
- **Purpose:** Climax, protagonist overcomes
- **Chapter Target:** 21-24
- **Key Elements:** Face villain/problem, demonstrate change

#### 15. Final Image
- **Purpose:** Contrast with opening, show transformation
- **Chapter Target:** 24
- **Key Elements:** Mirror opening image, show growth

## Act Breakpoints

```yaml
actBreakpoints:
  - "Catalyst" # End of Act 1 setup
  - "Break into Two" # Start of Act 2
  - "Midpoint" # Middle of story
  - "All Is Lost" # End of Act 2
  - "Break into Three" # Start of Act 3
```

## Application Guidelines

1. **Pacing:** Follow chapter targets for optimal pacing
2. **Theme:** Use B Story to carry thematic elements
3. **Character:** Show transformation through beat progression
4. **Conflict:** Escalate with each act
5. **Resolution:** Ensure Final Image contrasts with Opening Image

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Skipping or combining beats
- Placing beats at wrong chapter targets
- Not escalating stakes properly
- Missing the emotional arc
- Forgetting to show transformation
