## Example: Sarah

### Emotional Arc Summary

Sarah begins the story suppressing grief over her mother's death, channeling it into obsessive control. Through forced vulnerability, she learns that strength comes from allowing herself to feel, culminating in a cathartic breakdown that rebuilds her ability to connect.

### Dominant Emotions

- **Primary**: Suppressed grief (manifests as control)
- **Secondary**: Fear of abandonment
- **Conflict**: Need to appear strong vs need to be comforted

### Emotional State by Phase

- **Opening**: Controlled, distant, efficient
- **Rising Action**: Cracks appear under pressure
- **Midpoint**: Breakdown when control fails
- **Climax**: Allows herself to grieve publicly
- **Resolution**: Balanced strength with vulnerability

### Per-Chapter Emotional Beats

| Chapter | Emotional State | Trigger | Expression | Impact on Others |
|---------|-----------------|---------|------------|------------------|
| 1 | Controlled calm | Anniversary of death | Over-organizes | Team feels managed, not led |
| 3 | First crack | Photo found | Voice catches | Marc notices, files away |
| 7 | Defensive anger | Someone questions her | Lashes out | Julie pulls back |
| 12 | Full breakdown | Mission failure | Sobs, admits fear | Team rallies around her |
