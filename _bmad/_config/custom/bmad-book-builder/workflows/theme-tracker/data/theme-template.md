## Example: Trust vs Mistrust

### Core Question

Can people who have been hurt learn to trust again?

### Tension

The need for connection vs the fear of betrayal.

### Progression by Chapter Phase

- **Chapters 1-5**: Mistrust dominates (survival mode, isolation)
- **Chapters 6-10**: First openings (necessity forces cooperation)
- **Chapters 11-15**: Trust crisis (betrayal or sacrifice tests bonds)
- **Chapters 16-20**: Rebuilding (new dynamic emerges)
- **Chapters 21-25**: Resolution (trust earned or lost permanently)

### Character Connections

- **Marc**: Arc from mistrust → tentative trust → ultimate test
- **Julie**: Naive trust → betrayal → healed mistrust
- **Chen**: Pragmatic mistrust → selective trust

### Per-Chapter Progression

| Chapter | Theme Event | Character Impact | Next Step |
|---------|-------------|------------------|-----------|
| 1 | Marc refuses help from stranger | Establishes his isolation | Julie will challenge this |
| 2 | Julie trusts too easily | Gets deceived | Marc witnesses, reinforces his view |
| 3 | Forced cooperation | Both must rely on each other | Cracks in walls |
