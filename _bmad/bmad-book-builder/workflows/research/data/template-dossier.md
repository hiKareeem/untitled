## Example: Complete Dossier

```markdown
# Research Dossier: Emergency Medicine — 1970s France

## Dossier Metadata
- **Created:** 2026-01-25
- **Last Updated:** 2026-01-25
- **Source Count:** 5
- **Story Relevance:** Dr. Moreau is an emergency physician; Ch. 5, 8, 12, 15 feature emergency medical scenes
- **Reliability:** High

## Overview
Emergency medicine in 1970s France was in a transitional period, with modern emergency services being established and advanced medical equipment becoming more widely available in urban hospitals. This research supports accurate portrayal of Dr. Moreau's work and emergency scenes.

## Key Facts

### Emergency Room Technology
- **Fact 1:** Cardiac monitors and defibrillators were common in 1970s French hospitals
  - Source: https://www.historyofemergencymedicine.org/timeline/
  - Verification: ✅ Verified
  - Reliability: High

- **Fact 2:** CT scanners were introduced in 1971 but not widespread until late 1970s
  - Source: https://www.radiologyinfo.org/en/info.cfm?pg=ctscan
  - Verification: ✅ Verified
  - Reliability: High

- **Fact 3:** Basic ventilators and mechanical ventilation were available
  - Source: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC2664561/
  - Verification: ✅ Verified
  - Reliability: High

### Emergency Medical Services
- **Fact 4:** SAMU (Emergency Medical Aid Service) established in 1968
  - Source: https://www.samu-de-france.org/histoire
  - Verification: ✅ Verified
  - Reliability: High

- **Fact 5:** Term "paramedic" is Anglo-Saxon; French use "infirmier urgentiste"
  - Source: https://www.samu-de-france.org/roles
  - Verification: ✅ Verified
  - Reliability: High

## Technical Details

### Emergency Procedures
**Cricothyrotomy (Emergency Tracheotomy):**
- Last-resort procedure for complete airway obstruction
- Requires medical training or radio guidance from emergency services
- Risks: severing vital structures, bleeding, airway obstruction
- Should NOT be performed by laypeople except in absolute survival situations

**Emergency Response Times (1970s urban Paris):**
- SAMU response: 10-15 minutes (urban areas)
- Hospital arrival: 20-30 minutes from emergency call

## Common Misconceptions
- **Misconception:** Paramedics provided emergency care in 1970s France
  - **Reality:** SAMU provided emergency care with "infirmier urgentiste" not "paramedics"
  - Source: https://www.samu-de-france.org/histoire

- **Misconception:** Advanced imaging (CT, MRI) was readily available
  - **Reality:** CT scanners introduced 1971 but not widespread; MRI not invented until 1977
  - Source: https://www.radiologyinfo.org/en/info.cfm?pg=ctscan

## Story Applications
| Story Element | Chapter/Scene | How Research Applies |
|---------------|---------------|----------------------|
| Dr. Moreau profession | Ch. 5, 8, 12, 15 | Emergency physician role accurate for 1970s |
| Emergency room equipment | Ch. 5, Scene 2 | Cardiac monitors, defibrillators verified |
| Emergency tracheotomy | Ch. 12, Scene 3 | Procedure accurate but character needs medical background |
| Emergency response | Ch. 8, Scene 1 | Use SAMU terminology, not "paramedics" |

## Sources
1. **History of Emergency Medicine** — https://www.historyofemergencymedicine.org/timeline/ — High
2. **SAMU France History** — https://www.samu-de-france.org/histoire — High
3. **RadiologyInfo - CT Scan History** — https://www.radiologyinfo.org/en/info.cfm?pg=ctscan — High
4. **NCBI - Mechanical Ventilation History** — https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC2664561/ — High
5. **Medscape - Cricothyrotomy Procedure** — https://emedicine.medscape.com/article/83522-procedure — High

## Images/References
- [1970s French Hospital Ward](https://example.com/image-link)
- [Emergency Room Equipment Diagram](https://example.com/diagram-link)

## Notes for Author
**Critical Warnings:**
- Never use "paramedic" terminology — always reference SAMU
- Don't show advanced imaging (CT/MRI) as readily available
- Emergency procedures require medical background — don't have laypeople perform complex interventions

**Areas of Uncertainty:**
- Specific equipment models in 1970s French hospitals (minor detail)
- Exact response times for SAMU (10-15 minutes acceptable for story)

**Recommendations:**
- If writing more medical scenes, create specific dossiers for common procedures
- Research specific medical treatments relevant to story (e.g., trauma care, specific conditions)
```

## Dossier Creation Guidelines

### When to Create a Dossier

Create a research dossier when:
1. **Multiple facts needed** on the same topic (3+ facts)
2. **High-stakes domain** involved (medical, legal, technical, historical)
3. **Topic recurs** in multiple chapters or scenes
4. **Complex subject** that warrants ongoing reference
5. **Accuracy critical** for reader suspension of disbelief

### Dossier Naming

Use clear, descriptive names:
- ✅ Good: "Emergency Medicine — 1970s France"
- ✅ Good: "Surgical Instruments — Basic Procedures"
- ✅ Good: "Paris Metro — 1970s Routes and Stations"
- ❌ Avoid: "Medical Stuff"
- ❌ Avoid: "Research Notes 1"
- ❌ Avoid: "Topic A"

### Source Reliability Rating

**High Reliability:**
- Academic journals and publications
- University and research institutions
- Government and official organization websites
- Professional associations and societies
- Books from reputable publishers
- Established news outlets with editorial standards

**Medium Reliability:**
- Reputable magazines and publications
- Expert-written articles on established platforms
- Documentary films from reputable sources
- Museum and educational institution websites
- Well-referenced encyclopedias

**Low Reliability:**
- Personal blogs and forums (use with caution)
- Social media posts
- Unverified content
- Commercial sites with potential bias
- Sources with clear conflicts of interest

### Minimum Quality Standards

A complete research dossier must have:
- ✅ Minimum 3 reliable sources (Medium to High reliability)
- ✅ Minimum 10 verified facts organized by category
- ✅ At least 1 link to story element (character, chapter, location)
- ✅ All template sections completed
- ✅ Sources properly cited with URLs or references
- ✅ Verification status for each fact (✅ or ⚠️)
- ✅ Reliability rating for each fact and source

### Dossier Maintenance

**Update dossier when:**
- New information becomes available
- Story requirements change or expand
- Facts need re-verification
- New story applications are identified

**Cross-reference related dossiers:**
- Link to related topics in "Notes for Author" section
- Update Story Applications table as story evolves
- Track which chapters use each fact

**Archive outdated versions:**
- Keep dated backups if major updates needed
- Maintain validation history
- Note version changes in metadata
