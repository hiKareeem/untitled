# Hero's Journey — 12 Stages

**Origin:** Joseph Campbell's "The Hero with a Thousand Faces", adapted by Christopher Vogler

**Best for:** Epic adventures, fantasy, coming-of-age, transformational stories, mythic narratives

**Philosophy:** All stories share a universal pattern rooted in human psychology and mythology.

---

## The 12 Stages

### ACT I — DEPARTURE (0-25%)

**1. Ordinary World**
- Hero's normal life before the adventure
- Establishes what's at stake
- Shows hero's flaws and limitations
- Creates contrast with the special world
- *Question to ask:* What is your hero's everyday life like? What makes them relatable?

**2. Call to Adventure**
- A challenge or quest presents itself
- Disrupts the ordinary world
- Offers the hero a choice
- *Question to ask:* What event or message invites your hero to begin their journey?

**3. Refusal of the Call**
- Hero hesitates or refuses
- Fear, obligation, or self-doubt
- Shows the stakes are real
- *Question to ask:* Why would your hero be reluctant to answer the call?

**4. Meeting the Mentor**
- Wise figure provides guidance, training, or gifts
- May be a person, book, or inner voice
- Prepares hero for the journey
- *Question to ask:* Who or what gives your hero the wisdom or tools they need?

**5. Crossing the Threshold**
- Hero commits to the adventure
- Leaves the ordinary world behind
- Point of no return
- *Question to ask:* What event marks your hero's irreversible commitment?

### ACT II — INITIATION (25-75%)

**6. Tests, Allies, Enemies**
- Hero navigates the special world
- Learns the new rules
- Makes friends and foes
- Skills are tested
- *Question to ask:* Who helps your hero? Who opposes them? What challenges do they face?

**7. Approach to the Inmost Cave**
- Hero prepares for the major challenge
- May involve a second threshold
- Tension builds
- *Question to ask:* How does your hero prepare for the ordeal? What obstacles guard the prize?

**8. Ordeal**
- Hero faces their greatest fear
- Near-death experience (literal or metaphorical)
- Transformation begins
- *Question to ask:* What is the life-or-death crisis your hero must face?

**9. Reward (Seizing the Sword)**
- Hero gains what they sought
- May be treasure, knowledge, or reconciliation
- Celebration before the journey home
- *Question to ask:* What does your hero gain from surviving the ordeal?

### ACT III — RETURN (75-100%)

**10. The Road Back**
- Hero begins return to ordinary world
- May be chased or face complications
- Recommitment to the journey
- *Question to ask:* What challenges does your hero face on the way home?

**11. Resurrection**
- Final test — hero must use everything learned
- Climax of the story
- Hero is transformed/reborn
- *Question to ask:* How is your hero tested one final time? How do they prove they've changed?

**12. Return with the Elixir**
- Hero returns home transformed
- Brings gift for the community
- Ordinary world is changed
- *Question to ask:* What does your hero bring back? How is their world different now?

## Character Archetypes

The Hero's Journey often includes these archetypes:

| Archetype | Role | Your Story |
|-----------|------|------------|
| **Hero** | Protagonist on the journey | |
| **Mentor** | Provides guidance and gifts | |
| **Herald** | Announces the call to adventure | |
| **Threshold Guardian** | Tests hero at boundaries | |
| **Shapeshifter** | Character whose loyalty is uncertain | |
| **Shadow** | Antagonist, dark mirror of hero | |
| **Trickster** | Comic relief, catalyst for change | |
| **Allies** | Companions who aid the hero | |

## Mapping to Phase Structure

| Stage | Typical Phase Placement |
|-------|------------------------|
| Ordinary World | PHASE 1 |
| Call + Refusal + Mentor | PHASE 1-2 transition |
| Crossing Threshold | PHASE 2 start |
| Tests, Allies, Enemies | PHASE 2 |
| Approach + Ordeal | PHASE 2-3 transition |
| Reward | PHASE 3 |
| Road Back + Resurrection | PHASE 4 |
| Return with Elixir | PHASE 4 end |
