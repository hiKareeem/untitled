# Snowflake Method — Progressive Complexity

**Origin:** Randy Ingermanson's "How to Write a Novel Using the Snowflake Method"

**Best for:** Complex plots, multiple POV stories, authors who prefer bottom-up planning, detailed outliners

**Philosophy:** Start simple, add complexity in layers. A novel grows like a snowflake crystal.

---

## The 10 Steps

### STEP 1: One-Sentence Summary
- Summarize your novel in one sentence (15-25 words)
- No character names — just roles
- Focus on the big picture
- *Example:* "A disabled girl discovers she's the heir to a magical kingdom, but must defeat her evil aunt to claim her birthright."

## Mapping to Phase Structure

The Snowflake Method doesn't use traditional "phases" — instead, it builds complexity progressively:

| Snowflake Step | Phase Equivalent |
|----------------|------------------|
| Steps 1-2 | High-level structure (Acts) |
| Steps 3, 5, 7 | Character development |
| Steps 4, 6 | Phase breakdown |
| Steps 8-9 | Scene-level detail |

**For Foundation workflow:**
- Use Steps 1-2 to establish premise and overall structure
- Use Step 4 (one-page summary) as the basis for phases
- Each paragraph in the four-page synopsis becomes a phase
