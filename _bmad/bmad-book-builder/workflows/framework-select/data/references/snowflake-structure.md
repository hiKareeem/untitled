# Snowflake Method: Complete Framework Structure

## Framework Overview

**Name:** Snowflake Method
**Type:** Recursive-organic development
**Total Steps:** 10
**Phases:** 3 (Foundation, Characters/Structure, Planning/Drafting)
**Origin:** Randy Ingermanson

## Complete Step Structure

### Phase 1: Foundation (Steps 1-2)

#### 1. One-Sentence Summary
- **Purpose:** Story concept in 15 words or less
- **Phase:** Foundation
- **Output:** `sentence-summary.md`
- **Key Elements:** Protagonist, conflict, stakes
- **Guideline:** Who, what, why - in one sentence

#### 2. One-Paragraph Summary
- **Purpose:** Expand to 5 sentences: setup, disasters, ending
- **Phase:** Foundation
- **Output:** `paragraph-summary.md`
- **Structure:**
  - Sentence 1: Setup (introduce characters and setting)
  - Sentence 2: Major disaster 1
  - Sentence 3: Major disaster 2
  - Sentence 4: Major disaster 3
  - Sentence 5: Ending

### Phase 2: Characters & Structure (Steps 3-7)

#### 3. Character Sheets
- **Purpose:** One-page for each major character
- **Phase:** Characters
- **Output:** `character-sheets/`
- **Key Elements:**
  - Name, role, motivation
  - Story goal
  - Conflict/obstacles
  - Epiphany/what they learn
  - One-paragraph summary

#### 4. One-Page Synopsis
- **Purpose:** Expand paragraph to full page
- **Phase:** Structure
- **Output:** `synopsis-one-page.md`
- **Guideline:** Expand each sentence from step 2 into a full paragraph

#### 5. Character Descriptions
- **Purpose:** Full character bible
- **Phase:** Characters
- **Output:** `character-bible/`
- **Key Elements:**
  - Full character description
  - Storyline summary
  - Motivation and goals
  - Conflict and epiphany
  - Relationship to other characters

#### 6. Synopsis Expansion
- **Purpose:** Character storylines, subplots
- **Phase:** Structure
- **Output:** `synopsis-expanded.md`
- **Guideline:** Expand to 4-6 pages, include all major subplots

#### 7. Character Charts
- **Purpose:** Spreadsheets with character details
- **Phase:** Characters
- **Output:** `character-charts.xlsx`
- **Columns:** Name, role, goal, conflict, epiphany, storyline

### Phase 3: Planning & Drafting (Steps 8-10)

#### 8. Scene List
- **Purpose:** All scenes in spreadsheet format
- **Phase:** Structure
- **Output:** `scene-list.xlsx`
- **Columns:** Scene number, POV character, location, action, conflict, outcome

#### 9. Scene Planning
- **Purpose:** Narrative description of each scene
- **Phase:** Planning
- **Output:** `scene-plans/`
- **Guideline:** 1-2 pages per scene, narrative description

#### 10. First Draft
- **Purpose:** Write the novel
- **Phase:** Drafting
- **Output:** `chapters/`
- **Guideline:** Use scene plans, write linearly or by scene

```yaml
actBreakpoints:

  - "Scene List" # Structure complete
```

## Application Guidelines

1. **Recursive Process:** Each step expands the previous
2. **Character-First:** Develop characters deeply before plotting
3. **Organic Discovery:** Let story evolve through iterations
4. **Spreadsheet Tools:** Use spreadsheets for tracking
5. **Flexible Order:** Can adapt steps as needed

## Key Principles

- **Start Small:** Begin with one sentence, expand gradually
- **Character-Driven:** Characters determine plot
- **Iterative:** Keep refining and expanding
- **Organic:** Let story emerge naturally
- **Structured Planning:** Use tools to track complexity

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Rushing through foundation steps
- Not developing characters deeply enough
- Skipping spreadsheet organization
- Trying to write too soon
- Not following the recursive pattern
- Over-planning (analysis paralysis)
