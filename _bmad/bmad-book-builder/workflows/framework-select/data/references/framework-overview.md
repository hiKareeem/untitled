# Framework Overview

This document provides comprehensive information about all available narrative frameworks for the Framework Selection workflow.

## Available Frameworks

### 1. Save the Cat

**Type:** Beat-sheet structure
**Total Beats:** 15
**Acts:** 4
**Best For:** Commercial fiction, genre fiction, stories with clear external goals
**Origin:** Blake Snyder's screenwriting methodology adapted for novels

**Key Characteristics:**
- Precise pacing with specific beat placements
- Clear protagonist goal and obstacles
- Strong commercial appeal
- Predictable structure that readers expect

**Ideal For:**
- Thrillers, mysteries, romance
- Action-adventure stories
- YA and middle grade
- Genre fiction with clear external stakes

### 2. Hero's Journey

**Type:** Stage-based journey
**Total Stages:** 12
**Phases:** 3 (Departure, Initiation, Return)
**Best For:** Fantasy, adventure, sci-fi, transformation arcs
**Origin:** Joseph Campbell's monomyth structure

**Key Characteristics:**
- Mythic/epic storytelling
- Clear transformation arc
- Hero-villain dynamics
- Cross-cultural resonance

**Ideal For:**
- Fantasy and sci-fi epics
- Adventure stories
- Coming-of-age narratives
- Stories with mythic elements

### 3. Snowflake Method

**Type:** Recursive-organic development
**Total Steps:** 10
**Phases:** 3 (Foundation, Characters/Structure, Planning/Drafting)
**Best For:** Character-driven stories, literary fiction, ensemble casts
**Origin:** Randy Ingermanson's iterative development method

**Key Characteristics:**
- Character-first approach
- Organic story development
- Recursive refinement
- Spreadsheet-based planning

**Ideal For:**
- Literary fiction
- Character-driven narratives
- Ensemble cast stories
- Authors who prefer organic development

### 4. Custom Framework

**Type:** User-defined structure
**Flexibility:** Complete creative control
**Best For:** Experienced authors, experimental narratives, unique approaches

**Key Characteristics:**
- Fully customizable
- Can blend multiple frameworks
- Requires author to document approach
- Foundation uses as flexible guide

**Ideal For:**
- Experienced authors with established methods
- Experimental or non-traditional narratives
- Stories blending multiple genres
- Authors with specific structural preferences

## Selection Criteria

### Choose Save the Cat if:
- Story has clear external goal
- Pacing is critical
- Genre benefits from beat structure
- Protagonist has clear arc
- Commercial appeal is important

### Choose Hero's Journey if:
- Story has mythic/epic elements
- Transformation arc is central
- Hero-villain dynamic exists
- Fantasy/adventure genre
- Cross-cultural resonance desired

### Choose Snowflake Method if:
- Story is character-driven
- Multiple POV characters
- Literary fiction style
- Organic development preferred
- Ensemble cast present

### Choose Custom Framework if:
- Author is experienced
- Story is experimental/non-traditional
- Multiple genres blended
- Specific structural preferences exist
- Established personal method
