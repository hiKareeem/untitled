# Anti-Slop Checklist

**Purpose:** 24 patterns to detect and eliminate AI-like writing patterns.
**Source:** Humanizer framework (based on Wikipedia's Signs of AI writing)

---

## Content Patterns (1-6)

### 1. Significance Inflation
**Pattern:** "marking a pivotal moment in the evolution of..."
**Fix:** Use specific, concrete descriptions instead of inflated importance

### 2. Notability Name-Dropping
**Pattern:** "cited in NYT, BBC, FT, and The Hindu"
**Fix:** Only mention sources if directly relevant to the narrative

### 3. Superficial -ing Analyses
**Pattern:** "symbolizing... reflecting... showcasing..."
**Fix:** Show, don't tell. Use concrete actions and details

### 4. Promotional Language
**Pattern:** "nestled within the breathtaking region"
**Fix:** Use neutral, descriptive language

### 5. Vague Attributions
**Pattern:** "Experts believe it plays a crucial role"
**Fix:** Name specific sources or remove attribution

### 6. Formulaic Challenges
**Pattern:** "Despite challenges... continues to thrive"
**Fix:** Be specific about challenges and outcomes

---

## Language Patterns (7-12)

### 7. AI Vocabulary
**Pattern:** "Additionally... testament... landscape... showcasing"
**Fix:** Use author's natural vocabulary from style profile

### 8. Copula Avoidance
**Pattern:** "serves as... features... boasts" (instead of "is... has")
**Fix:** Use simple, direct verbs

### 9. Negative Parallelisms
**Pattern:** "It's not just X, it's Y"
**Fix:** State directly what something is

### 10. Rule of Three
**Pattern:** "innovation, inspiration, and insights"
**Fix:** Vary list lengths, use author's patterns

### 11. Synonym Cycling
**Pattern:** "protagonist... main character... central figure... hero"
**Fix:** Use consistent terminology per character

### 12. False Ranges
**Pattern:** "from the Big Bang to dark matter"
**Fix:** Use meaningful, connected ranges

---

## Style Patterns (13-18)

### 13. Em Dash Overuse
**Pattern:** "institutions—not the people—yet this continues—"
**Fix:** Match author's punctuation patterns from style profile

### 14. Boldface Overuse
**Pattern:** "__OKRs__, __KPIs__, __BMC__"
**Fix:** Use formatting sparingly, match author's style

### 15. Inline-Header Lists
**Pattern:** "__Performance:__ Performance improved"
**Fix:** Use natural prose or proper list formatting

### 16. Title Case Headings
**Pattern:** "Strategic Negotiations And Partnerships"
**Fix:** Use sentence case for natural headings

### 17. Emoji Usage
**Pattern:** "🚀 Launch Phase: 💡 Key Insight:"
**Fix:** Only use if author uses emojis in their style

### 18. Curly Quotes
**Pattern:** said "the project" (curly quotes)
**Fix:** Use straight quotes in fiction unless author prefers curly

---

## Communication Patterns (19-21)

### 19. Chatbot Artifacts
**Pattern:** "I hope this helps! Let me know if..."
**Fix:** Never include meta-commentary in narrative

### 20. Cutoff Disclaimers
**Pattern:** "While details are limited in available sources..."
**Fix:** Write confidently within the story world

### 21. Sycophantic Tone
**Pattern:** "Great question! You're absolutely right!"
**Fix:** Characters should have authentic reactions

---

## Filler and Hedging (22-24)

### 22. Filler Phrases
**Pattern:** "In order to", "Due to the fact that"
**Fix:** Use concise language ("to", "because")

### 23. Excessive Hedging
**Pattern:** "could potentially possibly"
**Fix:** Commit to statements in narrative

### 24. Generic Conclusions
**Pattern:** "The future looks bright"
**Fix:** End scenes with specific, meaningful details

---

## Usage Instructions

### During Draft (step-03)
- Keep this checklist in mind while generating
- Prefer author's vocabulary patterns from style profile
- Match sentence length distribution from style profile

### During Review (step-04)
- Style Coach scans for all 24 patterns
- Each violation includes: location, before/after, recommendation

### During Revision (step-06)
- Address flagged patterns systematically
- Preserve author's authentic quirks
- Re-check affected sections
