# Example: Good Extraction

This example demonstrates a high-quality extraction from a chapter with clear, specific information and proper continuity validation.

---

## Chapter 05 - Extraction

### Chronology
- **Day:** 12 (timeline explicit in chapter: "The twelfth day since their departure...")
- **Order:** Linear (no flashbacks)
- **Duration:** ~6 hours (morning to mid-afternoon)
- **Events:**
  - Morning: Discovery of the ancient map in the library archives
  - Noon: Confrontation between Jean and Marie about the expedition
  - Afternoon: Decision to proceed with the journey, team assembly begins

### Characters present

- **Jean Mercier**
  - **POV:** Yes (main POV for this chapter)
  - **Actions:** Discovers the map, argues with Marie, convinces the council to fund expedition
  - **Psychological state:** Excited but anxious - torn between scientific curiosity and awareness of danger. Quotes: "He felt his heart beating..." shows tension.
  - **Changes:** Shifts from passive researcher to active leader - takes initiative for the first time
  - **Relations:** Growing tension with Marie (disagreement on risk), gaining respect from council members

- **Marie Laurent**
  - **POV:** No
  - **Actions:** Challenges Jean's plan, argues for caution, ultimately concedes but remains skeptical
  - **Psychological state:** Worried, protective (concerned for Jean's safety)
  - **Changes:** From supportive colleague to protective guardian - reveals deeper care for Jean
  - **Relations:** Conflict with Jean (professional vs personal), respected by council for pragmatism

- **Councillor Dubois**
  - **POV:** No
  - **Actions:** Chairs the council meeting, approves funding after deliberation
  - **Psychological state:** Pragmatic, cautious authority figure
  - **Changes:** Initially skeptical, convinced by Jean's evidence
  - **Relations:** Respects both Jean and Marie

### Notable absences
- **Thomas** (Jean's assistant) - Notably absent from this critical decision despite being present in chapters 3-4. Chapter mentions "Thomas was away on assignment" (explains absence).

### Locations used

- **National Library - Secret Archives** [FIRST MENTION]
  - **Description:** Underground vault, dust-covered shelves, restricted access. "Smell of old paper and dampness"
  - **Events:** Map discovery, Jean spends 3 hours researching
  - **Resources:** Ancient maps, expedition journals from 1850s

- **Council Chamber**
  - **Description:** (matches existing bible entry - no new details)
  - **Events:** Heated debate, funding decision
  - **Characters present:** Jean, Marie, Councillor Dubois, 4 other council members (unnamed)

### Notable objects

- **The Lost Island Map** [FIRST MENTION]
  - **Status:** Found (discovered by Jean in archives)
  - **Changes:** From lost/forgotten to active plot device
  - **Significance:** Key to the entire expedition - shows route to the island. Chapter emphasizes "the key to everything"
  - **Location:** In Jean's possession at chapter end

- **Captain Moreau's Journal** [FIRST MENTION]
  - **Status:** Found (alongside the map)
  - **Changes:** Newly introduced
  - **Significance:** Provides historical context for the island, warnings about dangers
  - **Location:** Jean takes it from archives (chapter shows him "slipping the journal into his bag")

### Themes advanced

- **Curiosity vs Prudence** (existing theme)
  - **Progression:** Central conflict of this chapter - Jean embodies curiosity, Marie represents prudence. Debate externalizes this theme.
  - **Connections:** Directly linked to Jean's character arc (passive to active) and Marie's protective instincts
- **Symbols:** The map = curiosity/temptation, Marie's arguments = voice of caution

- **Price of Knowledge** [FIRST MENTION - emerging theme]
  - **Progression:** Introduced through council's debate - "What cost are we willing to pay for knowledge?"
  - **Connections:** Foreshadows future sacrifice/consequences
- **Symbols:** The funding (literal cost), Jean's anxiety (psychological cost)

### Inconsistencies detected
✅ **No inconsistencies detected**

- Timeline consistent with Chapter 4 ending (Day 11 evening → Day 12 morning)
- Jean's location: Was in his apartment end of Ch4 → library morning Ch5 (plausible overnight)
- Marie's skepticism consistent with her established personality (bible: "always prudent")
- Council decision aligns with established world rules (funding requires council approval, mentioned Ch2)

### Uncertain extractions
✅ **All extractions are clear**

- Chapter provides explicit timeline markers ("the twelfth day", "the morning", "around noon")
- Character emotions clearly described through internal monologue (POV) and dialogue
- Object significance explicitly stated by narrator
- No ambiguous references or vague time markers

## Why This Is a Good Extraction

**Specificity:**
- Exact day number (12), time spans (6 hours, 3 hours research)
- Named locations with descriptive details
- Clear character actions and motivations

**Continuity Awareness:**
- Checked timeline against Chapter 4
- Verified character locations make sense
- Noted Thomas's absence with chapter's explanation
- Cross-referenced Marie's personality with existing bible

**First Mentions Identified:**
- New location (Secret Archives)
- New objects (map, journal)
- New theme (Price of Knowledge)

**Psychological Depth:**
- Jean's internal conflict captured (excited but anxious)
- Marie's underlying motivation revealed (care for Jean, not just caution)
- Supported with quotes from chapter

**Thematic Connections:**
- Linked character actions to themes
- Identified symbolic elements (map = temptation)
- Noted emerging theme that foreshadows future story

**Clear Validation:**
- Explicit statement of no conflicts
- Explanation of why extractions are confident
- Shows verification was performed (not just assumed clean)

## Extraction Quality Checklist

- ✅ Timeline explicit and verified
- ✅ All POV characters have psychological depth
- ✅ Notable absences explained
- ✅ New entities marked [FIRST MENTION]
- ✅ Continuity checked against previous chapters
- ✅ Themes connected to character/plot
- ✅ Supported by chapter text (quotes when helpful)
- ✅ No hallucinations or assumptions
- ✅ Ambiguities flagged (none in this case)
- ✅ Conflicts noted (none in this case)
